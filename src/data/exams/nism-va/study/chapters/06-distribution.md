## 🎯 Summary Card

Two prerequisites to be a distributor: **NISM Series V-A certification + AMFI ARN**. Min age **18**. ARN expires every **3 years**. **Trail commission only** in India (upfront banned). Distributors cannot give holistic advice (that's RIA territory). **No commission on self-investments**. Trail = Annual rate × AUM ÷ 365 (daily). Online ARN process only since **1 October 2024**.

## 📖 Core Content

### Two prerequisites

1. **NISM Series V-A Certification**
   - Pass certification exam
   - Exemption: persons aged ≥50 OR with 10 years selling experience as of 31 May 2010 → CPE classroom credits

2. **AMFI Registration Number (ARN)**
   - Apply online via AMFI website
   - Min age: **18**
   - For institutions: institution registers; employees obtain EUIN
   - W.e.f. **1 October 2024**: ARN/EUIN applications online only

### ARN vs EUIN

| | ARN | EUIN |
|---|---|---|
| Issued to | Distributor (individual / firm / institution) | Employee of an institution |
| In application form | Yes | Yes (alongside institution's ARN) |

### Trail Commission — only model permitted

SEBI mandates **full trail model**. Upfront commission and upfronting of trail are NOT permitted (in cash, kind, sponsorships).

**Mechanics:**

- % of net assets attributable to units sold
- Calculated on daily balances
- Paid monthly or quarterly
- Continues as long as investor's money is in fund
- Daily formula: **Trail = (AUM × Annual rate) ÷ 365**

> **Critical:** NO commission for distributor's own investments (self-investment).

**Mandatory disclosure:** Distributors must disclose all commissions across competing schemes from all AMCs.

### Additional commission — B-30 cities

SEBI permits additional commission for distributors mobilizing investments from new investors in **B-30 cities** (cities beyond top 30) and women investors (financial inclusion).

### Distributor vs Investment Adviser (RIA)

| | MFD | RIA |
|---|---|---|
| Registration | AMFI (ARN) | SEBI (RIA license) |
| Charges fee from | AMC (commission) | Client directly |
| Advice scope | Cannot give holistic advice | Holistic across products |
| Advisory fee | NO | YES |
| Liability | Disclosure-based | Higher (fiduciary) |

### Nomination by distributor

Distributor may nominate person to receive their commission upon demise. Nominee doesn't need separate ARN.

### Change of distributor

- New ARN replaces old ARN
- Trail commission flows to new distributor
- No fee or charge to investor
- If investor goes **Direct Plan** → trail stops

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **Two prerequisites — "NA":** **N**ISM cert + **A**RN. (Easy: failing to remember = "N/A" for distributorship.)
- **Min age 18, ARN every 3 years:** "Adult (18) gets a 3-year license."
- **Trail formula — "ART/365":** **A**UM × **R**ate × **T**ime / 365. (Or just: annual rate divided by days in year.)
- **Self-investment commission rule — "Don't pay yourself twice":** you can't earn from your own investment.
- **MFD vs RIA — "MFD = Many Fund Distributor (no advice), RIA = Retainer Independent Adviser (no commission)"**.
- **B-30 + Women = additional commission** — financial inclusion lever.
- **Direct Plan = no trail:** direct investor pays no distributor, distributor earns nothing.
