## 🎯 Summary Card

**NAV = Net Assets ÷ Units outstanding**. AMC is responsible for fair valuation. Valuation policy reviewed annually. TER cap on first ₹500 Cr: **2.25%** (equity), **2.00%** (other) — slabs reduce as AUM grows; floor 1.05%/0.80% beyond ₹50,000 Cr. **Index/ETF cap 1%**. **Entry load banned since 1 Aug 2009**. Exit load goes back to scheme. Direct Plan TER < Regular Plan TER (no distribution cost), different NAVs, same portfolio. Segregated portfolio (side-pocket) created on a credit event.

## 📖 Core Content

### Fair valuation principles

- Valuation must reflect realizable value in good faith and true & fair manner
- Valuation policy reviewed at least annually
- Documented rationale for deviations
- Responsibility: **AMC** (always)

### NAV calculation

**NAV = Net Assets ÷ Units outstanding**

Net Assets = Market value of investments + Receivables (accrued income, sale receivables) − Liabilities (accrued expenses, fees payable, purchases payable).

Long form:
NAV = (Interest + Dividend + Realized gains + Valuation gains − Realized losses − Valuation losses − Expenses + carry-forward + initial capital) ÷ Units outstanding.

### TER limits — open-ended (other than FoF/Index/ETF)

| AUM Slab (₹ Cr) | Equity | Other |
|---|---|---|
| First 500 | **2.25%** | **2.00%** |
| Next 250 | 2.00% | 1.75% |
| Next 1,250 | 1.75% | 1.50% |
| Next 3,000 | 1.60% | 1.35% |
| Next 5,000 | 1.50% | 1.25% |
| Next 40,000 | −0.05% per ₹5,000 Cr | −0.05% per ₹5,000 Cr |
| Balance | **1.05%** | **0.80%** |

### Other TER caps

| Scheme | Cap |
|---|---|
| FoF (Liquid/Index/ETF only) | 1.00% |
| FoF (≥65% in equity-oriented) | 2.25% |
| FoF (other) | 2.00% (max 2× weighted-avg of underlying TER) |
| Index/ETF (standalone) | **1.00%** |
| Close-ended/Interval (equity) | 1.25% |
| Close-ended/Interval (other) | 1.00% |

### Additional charges allowed

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Brokerage — cash market | Up to 0.12% of trade value |
| Brokerage — derivatives | Up to 0.05% of trade value |
| Additional expense if exit load levied | Up to 0.05% of daily net assets |

### Direct Plan vs Regular Plan

| | Direct | Regular |
|---|---|---|
| TER | Lower (no distribution) | Higher (incl distribution) |
| Access | Direct to AMC | Via distributor |
| NAV | Different | Different |
| Portfolio | Same | Same |
| Trail commission | None | Yes |

> AMC cannot offer separate plans beyond Direct/Regular based on expense ratio.

### Entry / Exit Load

- **Entry Load: BANNED** since 1 August 2009
- **Exit Load:** applicable on redemption within specified period
- GST on exit load deducted from exit load; net credited to scheme
- Exit load proceeds go back to scheme (benefit to remaining unitholders)
- Schemes without exit load → cannot charge the additional 0.05% expense

### TER disclosure

Daily disclosure on AMC + AMFI websites in downloadable spreadsheet.

### Segregated portfolio (side-pocketing)

Created on a credit event (downgrade) on debt security. Defaulting security moved to "segregated portfolio." Existing investors get units of segregated portfolio. New investors don't have exposure. NAV calculated separately. AMC **cannot** charge investment management fees on segregated portfolio.

### Distributable reserves (for dividend)

- All accrued profits available
- Valuation gains: IGNORED (only realized gains)
- Valuation losses must be adjusted
- Trustees decide quantum + record date
- Up to monthly frequency, trustees can delegate to AMC

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **TER first slab — "2.25 / 2.00":** equity 2.25%, other 2.00%, on first ₹500 Cr. (Equity is always 0.25% higher than Other across all slabs.)
- **TER reduces as AUM grows — "fund size discount":** bigger fund = lower TER. Each slab reduces by ~0.25%.
- **Index / ETF cap = 1%:** passive funds are cheap because there's no active management.
- **FoF cap rule:** Liquid/Index/ETF FoF = 1% · Equity FoF = 2.25% (same as direct equity) · Mixed = 2.00% but capped at 2× underlying weighted-avg.
- **Entry load banned 2009, exit load goes to scheme:** "Exit pays the rest (of unitholders)."
- **Brokerage caps — "0.12 cash, 0.05 derivatives":** cash market is more expensive than derivatives.
- **Direct vs Regular — "Same portfolio, different NAV":** TER difference compounds over years.
- **Side pocket — "credit event creates a separate room":** existing investors get a unit in the room; AMC can't charge rent.
