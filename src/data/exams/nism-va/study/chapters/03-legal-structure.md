## 🎯 Summary Card

Indian MFs are trusts with a **3-tier structure: Sponsor → Trustees → AMC**, plus Custodian (holds assets) and RTA (handles unit records). Sponsor needs **5 yrs in financial services**, positive net worth in last 5 yrs, **₹10 Cr profit in immediately preceding year**. AMC needs **≥50% independent directors**. Trustees need **≥2/3 independent**. AMFI is the industry body that issues ARN.

## 📖 Core Content

### The 3-tier structure

```
SPONSOR (sets up the fund)
   ↓ creates
TRUST (the mutual fund itself)
   ↓ governed by
TRUSTEES (protect investor interest)
   ↓ appoint
AMC (Asset Management Company — runs schemes)
```

Plus support: **Custodian** (holds assets) + **RTA** (unit records).

### Sponsor — eligibility (memorize the 4 criteria)

1. **5 years** in financial services business
2. **Positive net worth** in each of immediately preceding 5 years
3. Net worth in immediately preceding year ≥ AMC's minimum capital
4. **Profit in 3 of last 5 years**, including immediately preceding year, with **≥₹10 crore profit** in immediately preceding year

### Trustees — key facts

- Application requires prior SEBI approval
- AMC, its directors/officers/employees → NOT eligible as trustees
- If trustee is a company → ≥4 directors, with **≥2/3 independent**
- File half-yearly reports to SEBI
- **75% of unitholders** can pass resolution to wind up scheme
- **75% of trustees** can terminate AMC

### AMC — key facts

- Appointed with prior SEBI approval
- **≥50% directors must be independent**
- Termination: by majority of trustees OR 75% of unitholders, with prior SEBI approval
- Day-to-day operations are AMC's responsibility
- Compliance Officer reports directly to head of AMC

### Custodian — key facts

- Registered under SEBI (Custodian) Regulations 1996
- If sponsor/associates control 50%+ voting OR 50%+ directors of custodian → cannot appoint (without specific conditions met)
- Tracks corporate actions (dividends, bonuses, rights)

### AMFI

- Industry body for AMCs
- Issues **ARN** (AMFI Registration Number) to distributors
- Maintains Code of Ethics (AMCs) + Code of Conduct (Intermediaries)

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **3-tier hierarchy — "STAC":** **S**ponsor → **T**rust → **A**MC, with **C**ustodian on the side.
- **Sponsor criteria — "5-5-5-3-10":** 5 years in business, positive net worth 5 years, 5 years for profit window, 3 of those 5 with profit, ₹10 Cr profit in last year.
- **75% rule — twice:** 75% unitholders to wind up scheme. 75% trustees to remove AMC. The number 75 appears in all "drastic action" decisions.
- **Independence ratios — "AMC half, Trustee two-thirds":** AMC needs ≥50% independent. Trustees (when company) need ≥2/3 independent. Trustees more strict because they protect investors.
- **Custodian disqualification — the 50/50 rule:** 50% voting OR 50% directors from sponsor side = no go.
