## 🎯 Summary Card

Three mandatory documents: **SID** (scheme-specific), **SAI** (about the MF/AMC), **KIM** (summary, must accompany application). KIM contains performance for **1, 3, 5 yrs and since inception**. Fundamental attributes = type / objective / pattern / load. Changing them triggers **written communication + 30-day exit window + 2-newspaper notice**. Riskometer has **6 levels** (was 5; "Very High" added). Product label has **4 elements** (Nature, Objective, Riskometer, Disclaimer).

## 📖 Core Content

### The 3 mandatory documents

| Document | Contents | Periodicity |
|---|---|---|
| **SID** (Scheme Information Document) | Specific scheme info | Updated for material changes |
| **SAI** (Statement of Additional Information) | About the MF / AMC (single SAI for all schemes) | Updated periodically |
| **KIM** (Key Information Memorandum) | Summary of SID + SAI — accompanies every application form | Updated with each material change |

> Technical detail: SAI is part of the SID legally, though presented separately.

### SID contents

Cover page (MF name, scheme name, NFO open/close/reopen dates, face value) · Risks (general + scheme-specific) · Investment objective, asset allocation, strategy · Information about the Scheme · Load structure, expense ratio · Liquidity, redemption · How to Apply · NAV computation · Tax laws · Fundamental attributes.

### SAI contents

Constituents (sponsor, trustees, AMC, custodian, RTA, schemes launched in last 3 FY, key personnel) · How to Apply (detail) · Rights of unitholders · Investment Valuation Norms · Tax, Legal, General · Investor Grievance redressal.

### KIM contents

- Performance of scheme + benchmark over **1 year, 3 years, 5 years, since inception**
- Summary of SID + SAI

Must accompany **every** application form.

### Fundamental Attributes

The "core aspects" of a scheme. Changes trigger:

1. Written communication to all unitholders
2. Exit window of at least **30 days** at prevailing NAV without exit load
3. Public notice in **2 newspapers** (1 English national + 1 vernacular at AMC HQ)

Examples of fundamental attributes:

- Type of scheme (open vs close-ended)
- Investment objective
- Investment pattern
- Terms of issue (load, expense, repurchase)

### Riskometer — 6 levels

1. Low
2. Low to Moderate
3. Moderate
4. Moderately High
5. High
6. **Very High** (added later)

### Product Label — 4 elements

a. **N**ature of scheme (wealth creation / regular income, time horizon)
b. Brief investment **O**bjective + product type (Equity/Debt)
c. Level of risk via **R**iskometer
d. **D**isclaimer to consult financial advisor

### Where to access SID, SAI, KIM

- SEBI website (draft SIDs)
- AMC website (downloadable)
- AMFI website (all SAIs)
- Investors can demand printed SAI

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **Three documents — "SAK":** **S**ID (scheme), **S**AI (additional/AMC), **K**IM (key memorandum).
- **KIM = Key Information Memorandum** — think of it as the **trailer**, while SID is the full movie. SAI is the director's commentary.
- **Performance disclosure pattern — "1-3-5-since":** appears in KIM and in advertising for >3 yr schemes.
- **Fundamental attributes change → "30-30-2":** 30-day notice, 30-day exit window, 2 newspapers.
- **Riskometer 6 levels — "L-LM-M-MH-H-VH":** Low, Low-Moderate, Moderate, Moderate-High, High, Very-High. Linear up.
- **Product Label — "NORD" (4 elements):** **N**ature, **O**bjective, **R**iskometer, **D**isclaimer.
