## 🎯 Summary Card

MFs themselves are tax-exempt (**Section 10(23D)**). Tax falls on the investor. **Equity-oriented = >65%** in listed Indian equities. Holding period: equity-oriented = **>12 months** for LTCG; non-equity = **>24 months**. **NEW (post 23 July 2024) tax rates:** Equity STCG **20%**, Equity LTCG **12.5%** (with **₹1.25L exemption**). Non-equity = slab rate (MSR) for both. ELSS gives 80C deduction up to ₹1.5L (old regime), **3-year lock-in**. **STT 0.001%** on equity MF redemption (NIL on debt). Stamp duty **0.005% on issue, 0.015% on transfer**. Capital losses can carry forward **8 years**.

## 📖 Core Content

### MF income — tax position

- MF entity is fully exempt from income tax (**Section 10(23D)**)
- Tax falls on investor when income received as dividend OR when units redeemed (capital gains)

### Equity-oriented vs Non-equity (the critical classification)

- **Equity-oriented:** holds **>65% in equity shares listed on Indian recognized stock exchanges**
- **Non-equity-oriented:** all others
- A FoF investing in equity MFs = **non-equity** for tax (it holds units, not shares directly)

### Capital gains — holding periods

| Asset type | Long-term threshold |
|---|---|
| Listed securities (incl equity shares, equity-oriented MF units) | **>12 months** |
| All other assets (incl debt MF, gold MF, FoF, international MF) | **>24 months** |

### Capital gains tax rates (post 23 July 2024) — MEMORIZE

| Asset | STCG | LTCG | Holding for LT |
|---|---|---|---|
| Equity-oriented funds (>65% equity) | **20%** | **12.5%** | >12 months. Exemption: **₹1.25L/yr** |
| Debt-oriented funds (>65% debt) | MSR | MSR | (Slab rate for both) |
| Hybrid 35–65% domestic equity | MSR | 12.5% | >24 months |
| Other MFs (FoF, International) | MSR | 12.5% | >24 months |
| Equity ETFs | MSR | 12.5% | >12 months |
| Gold ETFs | MSR | 12.5% | >12 months |
| Debt ETFs | MSR | MSR | (Slab rate for both) |

> **MSR** = Marginal Slab Rate (your income tax slab).

### ELSS — Section 80C deduction (old tax regime only)

- Eligible: up to **₹1.5 lakh per year** per taxpayer (individuals + HUFs)
- **3-year statutory lock-in** from date of investment
- For SIP: each instalment locked separately for 3 years
- Joint holding: tax benefit to first holder only
- New tax regime: **NO 80C benefit**, but lock-in still applies

### Grandfathering of LTCG (equity)

- Pre-1 Feb 2018 gains exempt
- Cost of acquisition for tax = higher of (purchase price) or (NAV as on 31 January 2018)

### LTCG ₹1.25 lakh exemption (equity)

- Equity LTCG up to ₹1.25 lakh per year is tax-exempt
- Tax applicable only on LTCG above ₹1.25 lakh

### Dividend (IDCW) tax

- DDT abolished (Union Budget 2020)
- Dividend now taxed in investor's hands at slab rate
- TDS @ **10%** if dividend > ₹5,000 (for residents)

### Stamp Duty (effective 1 July 2020)

| Transaction | Rate |
|---|---|
| Issue of units (Purchase, Add Purchase, IDCW Reinvest, SIP, STP, DTP) | **0.005%** |
| Transfer of units (e.g., demat-to-demat) | **0.015%** |

### Securities Transaction Tax (STT)

| Transaction | Rate | Payable by |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase of equity-oriented MF units | NIL | — |
| Sale of equity-oriented MF units (delivery) | 0.001% | Seller |
| Sale of equity shares / business trust / equity MF (non-delivery) | 0.025% | Seller |
| Sale to MF (re-purchase) of equity-oriented MF | 0.001% | Seller |

> STT NOT applicable on debt MFs.

### TDS

- No TDS on redemption proceeds for resident investors (capital gains)
- TDS @ 10% on dividends > ₹5,000 even for residents
- Different rates for non-residents (per Income Tax Act or DTAA — whichever lower)

### GST

- GST on **investment management & advisory fees:** charged to scheme **over and above TER limit**
- GST on **other fees:** within TER limit
- GST on **exit load:** deducted from exit load; net credited to scheme
- GST on **brokerage:** within TER limit
- GST on **distributor commission:** distributor pays; NOT charged to scheme

### Set-off rules

- Capital loss CANNOT be set off against any other head of income (e.g., salary)
- **STCL** can be set off against STCG OR LTCG
- **LTCL** can ONLY be set off against LTCG
- Unabsorbed capital loss carried forward up to **8 years**

### Bonus stripping

- Original units bought ≤3 months pre-bonus AND sold ≤9 months post-bonus
- Resultant capital loss is **DISALLOWED**
- Loss is treated as cost of bonus units instead

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **MF entity tax-exempt — "Section 10(23D) makes MF a pass-through pipe":** tax goes through to investor.
- **Equity-oriented = ">65% listed Indian equity":** 65 is the magic number for equity-orientation.
- **Holding period — "12 listed, 24 other":** 12 months for listed (incl equity MF), 24 months for everything else.
- **Tax rates picture (post-July 2024) — "12.5 LT, 20 ST, 1.25L free":**
  - Equity LTCG = 12.5% (after ₹1.25L exemption)
  - Equity STCG = 20%
  - Non-equity = your slab (MSR)
- **ELSS rule of thumb — "80-3-1.5":** 80% equity, 3-year lock-in, 80C up to ₹1.5L (old regime).
- **Grandfathering date — "31 Jan 2018":** higher of (purchase, NAV on this date) becomes cost basis.
- **Stamp duty — "5 issue, 15 transfer (per ₹1 lakh)":** Issue 0.005% (₹5 per ₹1L), Transfer 0.015% (₹15 per ₹1L).
- **STT on equity MF — "0.001%":** tiny, applies on sale only, NIL on debt MF.
- **Set-off rules — "STC opens, LTC closes":** ST loss → can offset ST or LT gain (open); LT loss → only LT gain (closed).
- **Carry forward — "8 years":** same as business losses under Income Tax.
- **Bonus stripping — "3 before, 9 after":** 3 months before bonus + 9 months after = loss disallowed.
- **TDS on dividend — "₹5,000 threshold, 10% rate":** simple.
- **GST split — "Mgmt fees outside TER, others inside":** investment management is the exception that goes over TER.
