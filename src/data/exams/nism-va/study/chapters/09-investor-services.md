## 🎯 Summary Card

NFO open: **min 3, max 15 days** (other than ELSS). Allotment within **5 business days**. NAV cut-off times: **Equity & Debt purchase/redemption 3:00 PM**; **Liquid/Overnight purchase 1:30 PM**. Cut-off is based on **funds availability** for purchase, not application time. Multiple bank accounts: **5 individual, 10 non-individual**. Up to **3 nominees** per folio. Cash transactions max **₹50,000/yr** per investor per MF. KYC mandatory for all. Systematic transactions: SIP, SWP, STP, DTP. **NEW Section 9.17:** voluntary Lock-in / Debit Freeze via MF Central platform.

## 📖 Core Content

> This is the most heavily tested chapter. Every section is testable.

### NFO process — three key dates

1. **NFO Open Date** — investors can start
2. **NFO Close Date** — last date to invest in NFO
3. **Scheme Re-opening Date** — open-ended scheme accepts purchases/redemptions at applicable NAV

Close-ended schemes have only Open & Close dates (don't re-open).

### NFO timelines (memorize)

| Item | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Min NFO open period | 3 working days |
| Max NFO open period (other than ELSS) | 15 days |
| Allotment OR refund | Within **5 business days** of NFO closure |
| Re-opening of open-ended scheme post NFO | Within 5 business days of allotment |
| Refund delay penalty | **15% p.a.** (AMC bears, not scheme) |
| Demat unit credit | 2 working days |

### NFO price

- Entry load banned → units allotted at face value (typically ₹10) during NFO
- Units = Investment ÷ ₹10
- Post-NFO ongoing price = applicable NAV

### Plans & Options

| Plans | Direct | Regular |
|---|---|---|
| TER | Lower | Higher |
| Distributor | None | Yes |
| NAV | Different | Different |

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| Growth | NAV captures all returns. Tax-deferred. |
| IDCW Pay-out | Periodic dividend to bank. NAV drops on record date. |
| IDCW Re-investment | Dividend reinvested at ex-dividend NAV. Units increase. |

> **IDCW** ("Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal") replaced "Dividend" terminology effective **1 April 2021**.

### Worked example — Growth vs Pay-out vs Reinvestment

100 units at NAV ₹10 → appreciates to ₹12 → MF declares 10% dividend (₹1/unit on ₹10 face value).

| Investor | Final units | Final value | Cash received |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A (Growth) | 100 | ₹1,200 | ₹0 |
| B (Pay-out) | 100 | ₹1,100 | ₹100 |
| C (Reinvestment, ex-NAV ₹11) | 109.09 | ₹1,200 | ₹0 |

Pre-tax all three earn ₹200. Post-tax differs.

### Cut-off times — MUST MEMORIZE

**Purchase / Switch-in:**

| Scheme | Cut-off | Applicable NAV |
|---|---|---|
| Equity & Debt (except liquid/overnight) | **3:00 PM** | NAV of business day **funds available** before cut-off (application time doesn't matter) |
| Liquid & Overnight | **1:30 PM** | If app + funds available before cut-off → closing NAV of day immediately preceding day of receipt |

**Redemption / Switch-out:**

| Scheme | Cut-off | Applicable NAV |
|---|---|---|
| Equity & Debt (except liquid) | 3:00 PM | Same day NAV (before cut-off); next business day (after) |
| Liquid funds | 3:00 PM | NAV of day immediately preceding next business day (before cut-off) |
| Overnight funds | 3:00 PM | Closing NAV of day immediately preceding next business day |
| Overnight funds (online) | **7:00 PM** | Extended cut-off |

> **CRITICAL TRAP:** For non-liquid purchases, NAV applies based on **when funds are available for utilization**, not when application is submitted. Exam favorite.

### Instant Access Facility (IAF)

- Only on **Liquid** schemes (not Overnight)
- Limit: **₹50,000 OR 90%** of latest investment value, whichever is lower
- Per day, per scheme, per investor

### Time stamping

- Done at Official Points of Acceptance (OPoA)
- AMC discloses OPoAs in SID + website
- Online: web server time
- If distributor receives but doesn't immediately submit → OPoA time matters

### KYC — central process

KYC required for:

- All investors (individual, non-individual, joint, NRI, PoA, guardian of minor)
- All transactions: new/additional purchase, switch, new SIP/micro-SIP/STP/DTP

Documents:

- PAN with photograph (mandatory, with limited exceptions)
- Identity proof, address proof, photograph
- **In-Person Verification (IPV)** — mandatory

KYC process steps:

1. Fill form + supporting docs
2. Verify against originals (or notarized copy)
3. IPV by authorized person
4. Upload to KRA (KYC Registration Agency)
5. KYC Identification Number issued
6. KYC done once → valid across SEBI-regulated entities

**CKYCR** — Central KYC Registry. Each customer assigned unique KYC Identifier; reporting entities download records using it.

**Online / e-KYC:** Aadhaar e-KYC via UIDAI accepted. e-Sign, DigiLocker, electronic signatures permitted. KUA / sub-KUA framework for SEBI intermediaries. Supreme Court (30 April 2025) — digital KYC must be accessible to persons with disabilities.

### KYC for special cases

| Case | KYC requirement |
|---|---|
| Minor | Done by guardian; proof of minor's age required |
| PoA holder | Both PoA holder AND issuer need KYC |
| NRI | Additional docs (overseas address, passport copy) |
| HUF | KYC of HUF + Karta |
| PEP (Politically Exposed Person) | Additional disclosures |
| UBO (Ultimate Beneficial Owner) | Required for ALL except individuals AND listed companies (or majority-owned subsidiaries) |

**FATCA / CRS:** for US/foreign tax compliance. Status change reported within **30 days**.

### Systematic transactions

**SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)**

- Constant amounts at regular intervals → Rupee Cost Averaging
- ELSS SIP lock-in: **each instalment 3 years from its own date**
- Modes: post-dated cheques, NACH, Direct Debit, Standing Instructions
- Enrollment: typically 15–30 days before first SIP date
- For minor: SIP runs only till date of attaining majority
- Pause facility available

**SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan)**

- Constant amount withdrawn at intervals; reduces risk of redeeming at trough
- Variable variant: only appreciation or only dividend transferred

**STP (Systematic Transfer Plan)**

- SWP from source + SIP into target (within same MF)
- Each leg = NAV of respective scheme on transfer date
- Exit load + tax apply on source redemption

**DTP (IDCW Transfer Plan)**

- Dividend earned in source transferred to target scheme
- Both schemes must be in same MF
- Investor must hold IDCW option of source
- All units in source IDCW option auto-covered (no partial enroll)
- Min app amount of target doesn't apply for DTP
- Auto-terminates when source unit holding becomes nil

**Triggers** — date / price / market level / % gain-loss. One-time facility; extinguishes after activation.

### Non-financial transactions

**Nomination**

- Up to **3 nominees** per folio with % allocation totaling 100%
- If % unspecified → equal division
- Single-holder folio: nomination required UNLESS investor expressly opts out
- Joint holding: ALL unitholders sign (regardless of mode of holding)
- Only individuals can nominate
- **Investments by minors CANNOT have a nomination**
- **PoA holder CANNOT nominate**

| Permitted | NOT permitted |
|---|---|
| Individuals (incl minors, NRIs) | Trust (except religious/charitable) |
| Central/State governments | Society |
| Local authorities | Body corporate |
|  | Partnership |
|  | HUF Karta |
|  | PoA holder |

**Pledge / Lien:** banks/NBFCs lend against unit pledge. Pledged units must be past lock-in. All unitholders sign pledge form.

**Transmission (death of unitholder):** units transmit to nominee/legal heir. AMFI prescribes standard formats.

### Change in status of special categories

**Minor turning Major**

- Folio frozen on majority date (no debits, no operations by guardian)
- Minor must complete: KYC, PAN updation, bank account in own name
- Submit MAM form + supporting docs
- SIP/SWP/STP suspended on majority if MAM not done
- Continued IDCW pay-out / reinvestment processed (not investor-initiated)

**NRI to Resident**

- NRO/NRE/FCNR(B) accounts cannot be operated as resident
- Open Resident Rupee Account; can also open RFC (foreign currency balances)
- Inform demat, MF, PAN

**Change of HUF Karta** (e.g., on death) — letter from new Karta + KYC + death certificate (attested) + bank certificate + indemnity bond from all coparceners.

### Investor turnaround times — MEMORIZE

| Service | Turnaround |
|---|---|
| NAV calculation & disclosure | Daily |
| NFO open period (other than ELSS) | Max 15 days |
| Allotment/refund post NFO | 5 business days |
| Re-opening for ongoing sale | 5 business days post allotment |

### Payment mechanisms

- Online: NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, NACH, internet banking, UPI
- Cheque: local; out-station only if "at par"; not stale (>3 months); not post-dated except future SIP instalments
- DD: only when no collection centre at investor's location
- ASBA: for NFO subscriptions (money blocked till allotment, earns interest till then)
- Cash: limited (see below)

**Cash transactions**

- Up to **₹50,000** per investor, per MF, per financial year
- Only resident individuals, sole proprietors, minors via guardian
- KYC required (PAN may not be needed for these small investors)

### Third-party payments — generally NOT allowed

3rd-party = payment from a bank account other than first holder's. Exceptions:

- **Parents/grandparents/related** paying for minor — up to ₹50,000 per regular purchase or per SIP instalment (KYC of payer + Third Party Declaration form)
- **Employer** paying for employee via payroll deductions
- **Custodian** on behalf of FPI
- **AMC** paying empaneled distributor in MF units (commission)

> Pre-funded DD ≥ ₹50,000 issued against cash → not accepted.

**2-Factor Authentication** (SEBI mandate) for subscription AND redemption. Pool accounts of brokers/clearing members discontinued for MF transactions.

### Unclaimed assets — MITRA platform

RTAs developed **Mutual Fund Investment Tracing and Retrieval Assistant (MITRA)**. Investors can search inactive/unclaimed folios industry-wide. SEBI also: norms for dormant accounts, mandatory contact info, mandatory nomination (or opt-out), simplified transmission, centralized demise reporting.

**Unclaimed redemption / dividend**

- Within 3 years: prevailing NAV
- After 3 years: NAV at end of 3-year period

### 9.17 Voluntary Lock-in / Debit Freeze (NEW — March 2026)

- SEBI introduced facility to voluntarily lock-in units as precaution against unauthorized redemption
- Voluntary debit freeze for demat AND non-demat (SoA) folios
- No units debited from such folios till unlock
- First phase: facility provided by RTAs through **MF Central** platform

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **NFO timelines — "3-15-5":** 3 days min open, 15 days max (non-ELSS), 5 days for allotment.
- **Cut-off times — "3 PM main, 1:30 PM liquid":** Equity & Debt purchase/redemption at 3 PM. Liquid/Overnight purchase at 1:30 PM. Online overnight redemption extended to 7 PM.
- **Critical trap reminder:** Purchase NAV depends on **fund availability**, not application time. Liquid/Overnight has narrowest window (1:30 PM) because they need the money to deploy same-day.
- **Bank accounts — "5 individual, 10 non-individual":** scale doubles for non-individual.
- **Nominees:** 3 max, only individuals can nominate, minor's investments **cannot** have nominee, PoA cannot nominate. Four rules; memorize as a unit.
- **Nominee permitted set — "Individual + Government bodies only":** corporates, partnerships, societies, HUF Kartas, trusts (other than religious/charitable) all blocked.
- **IDCW renamed April 2021:** Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal. Reflects that part of "dividend" is actually capital withdrawal.
- **Cash limit — "₹50,000 per investor per MF per FY":** triple constraint.
- **3rd-party allowed — "PECA":** **P**arent, **E**mployer, **C**ustodian, **A**MC.
- **ELSS SIP lock-in — "each instalment is its own clock":** 3 years from each SIP date.
- **Minor on majority — "MAM freezes the folio":** until Minor-to-Major form submitted, folio frozen.
- **KYC trap — "apply once, valid for all SEBI entities":** KRA framework means one KYC works for MF, demat, broking.
- **MITRA — "search before lost":** Mutual Fund Investment Tracing and Retrieval Assistant.
- **NEW (9.17) — "MF Central + Voluntary Lock":** investor-initiated debit freeze via MF Central. New SEBI March 2026 circular.
