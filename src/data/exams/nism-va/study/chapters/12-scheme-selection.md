## 🎯 Summary Card

Selection sequence: **Need → Risk profile (3D) → Asset allocation → Core/Satellite → Pick scheme**. Within debt: Overnight → Liquid → Ultra Short → ... → Long Duration by interest-rate risk. Within equity: Large → Large&Mid → Multi → Mid → Small by risk. Within hybrid: Arbitrage → Equity Savings → Conservative → Dynamic → Multi Asset → Balanced → Aggressive. Active vs Passive: passive cheaper (TER ≤1%), no alpha potential. For ELSS: **Growth + IDCW Pay-out only** (Reinvestment causes 3-yr lock-in on each reinvested dividend).

## 📖 Core Content

> This chapter pulls everything together. Easiest place to score.

### Step 1: Investor's Need

- Long-term appreciation → equity-heavy
- Periodic income → debt-heavy
- Liquidity → liquid funds
- Capital preservation → debt / conservative hybrid

### Step 2: Risk Profile (3D — from Ch 1)

1. **Need** to take risk
2. **Ability** to take risk (capacity, horizon)
3. **Willingness** to take risk (psychological)

### Step 3: Asset allocation

Investor's need + ability + horizon → asset class. Long horizon doesn't auto-equal equity (willingness matters).

### Step 4: Core & Satellite portfolio

| Component | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Core | Long-term, broad-market | Diversified equity, large-cap, mid-cap, multi-cap |
| Satellite | Tactical, short-term opportunities | Sector funds, thematic, gold, long-duration gilt |

Conservative: small satellite. Moderate: ~80/20 core/satellite. Aggressive: higher satellite.

### Risk-return hierarchies — MEMORIZE ALL FIVE

**Across MF categories (lowest → highest risk):**
Liquid → Debt → Hybrid → Equity

**Within debt (lowest → highest interest-rate risk):**
Overnight → Liquid → Ultra Short → Low → Short → Medium → Medium-Long → Long Duration

**Within debt (lowest → highest credit risk):**
Gilt → Banking & PSU → Corporate Bond → Credit Risk Fund

**Within equity (lowest → highest risk):**
Large Cap → Large & Mid Cap → Multi Cap → Mid Cap → Small Cap

**Within hybrid (lowest → highest risk):**
Arbitrage → Equity Savings → Conservative → Dynamic Asset Allocation → Multi Asset → Balanced → Aggressive Hybrid

**Diversification spectrum (lowest → highest concentration risk):**
Diversified → Focused → Thematic → Sector

### Active vs Passive selection logic

| Factor | Active | Passive |
|---|---|---|
| TER | Higher | Lower (≤1%) |
| Goal | Beat benchmark | Mirror benchmark |
| Risk source | Manager skill + market | Market only |
| Suitable for | Wants alpha, accepts risk | Wants market exposure simply |

### Open-ended vs Close-ended

| Factor | Open-ended | Close-ended |
|---|---|---|
| Liquidity | Daily, with AMC | Via stock exchange (often illiquid) |
| Pricing | NAV | Often at discount to NAV |
| Fund manager freedom | Constrained by liquidity | Better (no redemption pressure) |
| Investor inertia risk | More flow disruption | Less |
| Horizon | Flexible | Fixed |

### Diversified vs Sector / Thematic

- **Diversified equity** (multi/large/mid/small): lower risk, sector spread
- **Focused fund:** max **30 stocks**; higher risk than diversified
- **Sector fund:** single sector; cyclical performance; satellite portfolio
- **Thematic fund:** theme-based across sectors; less concentrated than sector

### Investment style — Growth vs Value

| Style | Approach | Suitable for |
|---|---|---|
| Growth | High-earnings-growth companies | Bull markets, accept higher volatility |
| Value | Undervalued (low P/E, P/B) | Long horizon, contrarian |
| Contra | Bets against market sentiment | Same as value |

> AMC offers either **Value** OR **Contra**, not both.

### Equity scheme selection criteria

1. Performance vs benchmark + peers
2. Portfolio: sector spread, top holdings, market cap
3. Fund age (longer = better evaluation)
4. Fund size (AUM): too small → high impact cost; too large → mid/small cap may struggle to deploy
5. Portfolio turnover: high = higher trading costs (also active management signal)
6. TER

### Debt scheme selection

1. Macaulay Duration matches horizon
2. YTM (current portfolio yield)
3. Credit quality (AAA, AA+, AA distribution)
4. Modified duration (rate sensitivity)
5. Expense ratio

### Selection within category (across AMCs) — apply in order

1. Performance: 1, 3, 5 yrs + since inception (vs benchmark, peers)
2. Risk-adjusted returns: Sharpe, Treynor, IR, Alpha
3. Standard deviation
4. Portfolio composition
5. Fund manager track record + tenure
6. AMC reputation
7. TER

### Selection of OPTIONS within a scheme

| Investor profile | Best option |
|---|---|
| Want growth, no income, tax-efficient | Growth |
| Want regular income, OK with slab-rate tax on dividends | IDCW Pay-out |
| Want compounding via reinvested units | IDCW Reinvestment |
| Lower TER, manage own decisions | Direct Plan |
| Need distributor support | Regular Plan |

> For **ELSS**: most AMCs offer only **Growth + IDCW Pay-out** (Reinvestment causes a 3-year lock-in on each reinvested dividend).

Tax efficiency: **Growth > IDCW** (tax deferment via compounding).

### Do's and Don'ts

**Do's**

- Match scheme to goal, risk profile, horizon
- Use Riskometer + Product Label as filter
- Compare across AMCs within same category
- Look at **rolling returns**, not just point-to-point
- Check fund manager tenure

**Don'ts**

- Don't pick by past returns alone
- Don't ignore expense ratios (compounds over years)
- Don't go by NFO craze (no track record)
- Don't recommend high-risk to low-risk-appetite investors
- Don't churn (move investor between schemes for commission)

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **Selection sequence — "NRACP":** Need → Risk → Allocate → Core → Pick.
- **Equity hierarchy — "Large to Small in 5 steps":** Large → L&M → Multi → Mid → Small.
- **Debt rate-risk hierarchy — "OLuLiM SMML L":** Overnight, Liquid, Ultra short, Low, Money market, Short, Medium, Medium-Long, Long. (Or just: shorter maturity = lower rate risk.)
- **Debt credit-risk hierarchy — "GBCC":** **G**ilt (lowest), **B**anking & PSU, **C**orporate Bond, **C**redit Risk (highest).
- **Hybrid hierarchy — "ASCDMBA":** **A**rbitrage → equity **S**avings → **C**onservative → **D**ynamic → **M**ulti Asset → **B**alanced → **A**ggressive. (Say it 3 times, sticks.)
- **Diversification — "DFTS":** **D**iversified → **F**ocused (≤30 stocks) → **T**hematic → **S**ector. Increasing concentration.
- **Active vs Passive — "Active beats, Passive mirrors":** TER difference compounds over decades.
- **ELSS option choice — "Growth or Pay-out, not Reinvestment":** each reinvested unit gets a 3-yr lock-in.
- **Don't-churn rule:** AMFI Code of Conduct prohibits moving investors for commission.
