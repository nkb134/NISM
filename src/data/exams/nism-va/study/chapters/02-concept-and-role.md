## 🎯 Summary Card

An MF is a **trust** — you invest THROUGH it, not in it. SEBI categorizes schemes into **5 broad categories with 37 sub-categories total**: Equity (11), Debt (16), Hybrid (6), Solution-Oriented (2), Other (2). Open-ended = continuous; close-ended = fixed maturity, must list on exchange (except ELSS); interval = NFO + transaction windows. ETFs trade like stocks with intraday pricing.

## 📖 Core Content

### Advantages — memorize the list

Professional management · Affordable diversification · Economies of scale · Transparency (SID/SAI/KIM/NAV) · Liquidity · Tax deferral (growth option) · Tax benefits (ELSS 80C) · Convenience (SIP/SWP/STP) · Investment comfort (one-time KYC) · Regulatory comfort (SEBI-monitored).

### Limitations

No portfolio customization (PMS gives this) · Choice overload · No control over costs (TER capped) · No guaranteed returns.

### SEBI's 5 broad scheme categories (memorize the counts)

| Category | Sub-categories |
|---|---:|
| Equity | 11 |
| Debt | 16 |
| Hybrid | 6 |
| Solution-Oriented | 2 (Retirement, Children's) |
| Other | 2 (Index/ETF, FoF) |

**Total: 37 sub-categories.**

### Equity sub-categories (11) — the critical numbers

| Sub-category | Min equity allocation |
|---|---|
| Multi Cap | **75%** total, 25% each in large/mid/small |
| Large Cap | **80%** in large cap |
| Large & Mid Cap | 35% large + 35% mid (=70% min) |
| Mid Cap | **65%** in mid cap |
| Small Cap | **65%** in small cap |
| Dividend Yield | 65% min equity |
| Value / Contra | 65% min equity (AMC offers EITHER, not both) |
| Focused | 65% min equity, **max 30 stocks** |
| Sectoral / Thematic | **80%** in sector/theme |
| ELSS | **80%** min equity, 3-year statutory lock-in |
| Flexi Cap | 65% min equity (dynamic across caps) |

### Cap definitions by full market cap (memorize verbatim)

- **Large Cap:** 1st – 100th company
- **Mid Cap:** 101st – 250th company
- **Small Cap:** 251st onwards

### Debt sub-categories (16) — Macaulay duration ranges

| Sub-category | Maturity / Duration |
|---|---|
| Overnight | 1 day |
| Liquid | up to 91 days |
| Ultra Short Duration | 3–6 months |
| Low Duration | 6–12 months |
| Money Market | up to 1 year |
| Short Duration | 1–3 years |
| Medium Duration | 3–4 years |
| Medium to Long Duration | 4–7 years |
| Long Duration | > 7 years |
| Dynamic Bond | Across duration |
| Corporate Bond | 80% min in AA+ and above |
| Credit Risk | 65% min in AA and below (excl AA+) |
| Banking & PSU | 80% min in banks/PSU/PFI/Municipal |
| Gilt | 80% min in G-secs (across maturity) |
| Gilt with 10-yr constant duration | 80% G-secs, Macaulay = 10 yrs |
| Floater | 65% min in floating rate |

### Hybrid sub-categories (7)

| Sub-category | Equity | Debt |
|---|---|---|
| Conservative Hybrid | 10–25% | 75–90% |
| Balanced Hybrid | 40–60% | 40–60% (no arbitrage) |
| Aggressive Hybrid | 65–80% | 20–35% |
| Dynamic Asset Allocation / Balanced Advantage | Dynamic | Dynamic |
| Multi Asset Allocation | min 3 classes, min 10% each | — |
| Arbitrage | 65% min equity | — |
| Equity Savings | 65% min equity, 10% min debt | — |

> AMC may offer either **Aggressive OR Balanced Hybrid**, not both.

### Solution-Oriented (2)

- **Retirement Fund** — lock-in: 5 years OR till retirement age
- **Children's Fund** — lock-in: 5 years OR till majority

### Other Schemes (2)

- **Index Fund / ETF** — min **95%** in tracked index
- **Fund of Funds (FoF)** — min **95%** in underlying fund

### One-scheme-per-category rule — the 3 exceptions

A category can have only one scheme, EXCEPT:

1. Index funds / ETFs tracking different indices
2. FoFs with different underlying schemes
3. Sector / thematic funds in different sectors / themes

### Open vs Close-ended vs Interval

| Feature | Open-ended | Close-ended | Interval |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maturity | None | Fixed | Fixed (with windows) |
| Buy/Sell | From MF anytime | NFO only; then exchange | NFO + transaction periods |
| Listing | Optional | Mandatory (except ELSS) | Mandatory |
| Transaction period | — | — | min 2 days |
| Interval period | — | — | max 15 days |

### ETFs

Trades on exchange like a stock · Intraday pricing · Passive (tracks index) · Demat required · Silver ETFs introduced (similar to Gold ETFs).

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **Sub-categories — "11-16-6-2-2 = 37":** Equity 11, Debt 16, Hybrid 6, Solution 2, Other 2. Add them: 37.
- **Cap definition trick — "1, 100, 250":** Large is the first 100, Mid is the next 150 (101–250), Small is everything after. Picture a podium with three steps.
- **Equity allocation pattern:**
  - **80% category** = the "narrow ones": Large Cap, Sectoral/Thematic, ELSS
  - **75%** = Multi Cap (the only odd one out)
  - **65%** = everything else (Mid, Small, Focused, Dividend Yield, Flexi, Value/Contra)
- **Hybrid mnemonic — "10-25, 40-60, 65-80":** Conservative (10-25%), Balanced (40-60%), Aggressive (65-80%). Three steps up.
- **ELSS shortcut:** 80% equity, 3-year lock-in, 80C (max ₹1.5L). Three numbers; all starts with 80 except 3.
- **One-scheme rule — "3 ways to copy":** Index trackers · FoFs · Sector funds. You can have multiple of each since each tracks something different.
- **Close-ended must list — except ELSS:** picture an ELSS investor "locked in" — locked in for 3 years means they can't trade it anyway, so listing isn't useful.
- **Interval scheme — "between 2 and 15":** 2 days transaction minimum, 15 days interval maximum, "or you can't trade in between."
