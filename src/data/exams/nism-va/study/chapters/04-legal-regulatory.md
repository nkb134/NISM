## 🎯 Summary Card

Four regulators in India: **RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA**. SEBI regulates MFs under the new **SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026** (replaced 1996 regs). Memorize investment limits: **10% per company** (equity & debt, with 12% extension), **25% sponsor associates equity cap**, **15% bank exposure** (extendable 20%), **10% REIT/InvIT total** (5% per single). Performance can only be advertised after **1 year**. CAGR for 1, 3, 5 yrs and since inception mandatory for >3-year schemes. **75% of unitholders** can wind up a scheme. **30-day exit window** for fundamental attribute changes.

## 📖 Core Content

### Regulators in India

| Regulator | Domain |
|---|---|
| RBI | Banking, NBFCs, payment systems, money market |
| SEBI | Securities markets, MFs, AMCs, distributors |
| IRDAI | Insurance |
| PFRDA | Pension (NPS) |

### SEBI's role for MFs

Operates under **SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026** (newly framed; replaces 1996 regulations referenced earlier).

Covers: registration of MFs/AMCs · disclosure (SID/SAI/KIM/portfolio/NAV) · categorization (2017 circular) · mergers/conversions · risk management · investment restrictions · advertisement code · grievance redressal · corporate debt market.

### Investment limits — HIGH-YIELD, memorize all

**General company exposure**

| Limit | Rule |
|---|---|
| Single company equity (per scheme) | Max **10%** of NAV |
| MF (across all schemes) ownership of single co. | Max **10%** of voting rights |
| Single company debt (per scheme) | Max **10%** of NAV, extendable to **12%** with Trustee + AMC Board approval |
| Sponsor associates equity (across MF) | Max **25%** of net assets |
| Single bank exposure (per scheme) | Max **15%** of NAV, extendable to **20%** |
| Unrated debt (per scheme) | Max **10%** of debt portfolio, min investment grade |
| ELSS — equity allocation | Min **80%** |

**REITs and InvITs**

| Limit | Rule |
|---|---|
| Per scheme — total ReIT/InvIT | Max **10%** of NAV |
| Per scheme — single REIT or InvIT | Max **5%** of NAV |
| MF (across schemes) — REIT/InvIT units | Max **10%** of issued units |

**Perpetual bonds (AT 1 / AT 2)**

| Limit | Rule |
|---|---|
| MF (single issuer) | Max **10%** of issuer's such bonds |
| Single scheme — total such instruments | Max **10%** of assets |
| Single scheme — single issuer | Max **5%** |

**Valuation specifics**

- AT 1 = deemed residual maturity 100 years (from 1 April 2023)
- AT 2 = contractual maturity
- AT 1 valued on Yield to Call (YTC) basis
- Close-ended debt schemes cannot invest in perpetuals (would exceed scheme maturity)

### Advertisement Code

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Performance advertising | Only if scheme is **>1 year old** |
| Schemes 1–3 yrs | Annualized yields if performance available for ≥7, 15, or 30 days |
| Schemes >3 yrs | **CAGR for 1, 3, 5 yrs and since inception** (mandatory) |
| Debt scheme benchmark | 10-year G-sec (general) |
| Conservative Hybrid | 10-year G-sec |
| Equity Savings | 10-year G-sec |
| Celebrity endorsement | Permitted at industry level only (not scheme-specific). Prior SEBI approval. |

### Specialized Investment Fund (SIF)

- Cannot use "SIF" in scheme name for **5 years** from SEBI approval
- Min investment: **₹10 lakh per investor** at PAN level

### Winding up & Exit Window

- **75% of unitholders** can pass resolution to wind up
- Exit window **≥ 30 days** for fundamental attribute changes
- Public notice in 2 newspapers (1 English national + 1 vernacular)

### Unclaimed redemption / dividend

- Within 3 years: paid at prevailing NAV
- After 3 years: paid at NAV as of end of 3-year period

### AMFI Code of Conduct (intermediaries) — major commitments

- Full disclosure on commissions, products, alternatives
- **No rebating** (passing back commission)
- No inducement via gifts/rebates
- Confidentiality of investor data
- Disclose conflicts of interest
- KYC compliance
- Avoid mis-selling

### Investor grievance — formal redressal

- Status of complaints on AMC website AND AMFI website
- Pending complaints can be ground for SEBI to refuse new scheme permission
- Accessible KYC mandated by Supreme Court (30 April 2025) for persons with disabilities

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **Regulators by mandate — "RSIP":** **R**BI (banking), **S**EBI (securities), **I**RDAI (insurance), **P**FRDA (pension).
- **The 10% rule appears 4 times — memorize as one block:**
  1. Single company equity (per scheme): 10%
  2. MF ownership of any single company: 10%
  3. Single company debt (per scheme): 10%
  4. ReIT/InvIT total per scheme: 10%
- **The 5/10/15/20/25 ladder:** 5% = single REIT/InvIT or single AT-1 issuer (per scheme) · 10% = single company equity/debt, total REIT/InvIT, AT-1 across MF · 15% = single bank (default) · 20% = single bank (extended) · 25% = sponsor associates equity.
- **Performance advertising — "1-3 rule":** <1 year → no ad; 1–3 years → short-term annualized yields OK; >3 years → CAGR 1/3/5/since-inception.
- **SIF — "10-5":** ₹10 lakh per PAN, can't say "SIF" in name for 5 years.
- **Exit window — "30-30":** 30-day window, double-newspaper public notice.
- **The 75% rule:** 75% unitholders to wind up a scheme; 75% trustees to terminate the AMC.
