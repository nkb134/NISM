## 🎯 Summary Card

Saving precedes investing. Evaluate any investment by **Safety, Liquidity, Returns**. Real return = Nominal − Inflation (can be negative). Five risk types: **inflation, liquidity, credit, market/price, interest rate**. Diversification kills company-specific risk, not market-wide risk. Risk profiling has 3 dimensions: **need, ability, willingness**. Asset allocation is **strategic** (target %, rebalance) or **tactical** (active shifts).

## 📖 Core Content

### Saving vs Investment

- **Saving** = reducing consumption (origin: same root as "safe").
- **Investment** = deploying saved money to earn returns.

### The three factors to evaluate any investment

**Safety, Liquidity, Returns.** Plus convenience, ticket size, taxability.

Returns = Current income (regular) + Capital appreciation (only on sale).

**Real rate of return = Nominal − Inflation.** If FD pays 7% and inflation is 8%, real rate is *negative*.

### Asset classes

Equity • Fixed Income • Real Estate (incl REITs/InvITs) • Commodities (Gold, Silver) • Hybrid • Others (Art, rare coins).

### The 5 investment risks

1. **Inflation risk** — purchasing power erosion.
2. **Liquidity risk** — can't convert to cash quickly.
3. **Credit risk** — borrower defaults; G-secs = lowest in country.
4. **Market & Price risk** — broad market vs company-specific. Diversification handles company-specific only.
5. **Interest rate risk** — bond prices move INVERSELY to rates. Longer maturity = bigger swing.

### Behavioural biases (know them by name)

Recency • Confirmation • Herd mentality • Anchoring • Loss aversion.

### Risk profiling — 3 dimensions

1. **Need** to take risk (do goals require it?)
2. **Ability** to take risk (capacity, horizon)
3. **Willingness** to take risk (psychological)

The distributor must balance all three when they conflict.

### Asset allocation — 2 approaches

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Strategic | Fixed target % allocation; rebalance on drift |
| Tactical (Dynamic) | Active shifts to exploit opportunities; for sophisticated investors |

> **SEBI rule:** passive breaches of allocation must be corrected within **30 business days** (except Index Funds, ETFs, Overnight Funds).

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **SLR for evaluation:** **S**afety, **L**iquidity, **R**eturns — same letters as the bank's SLR ratio.
- **The 5 risks → "I LCMP my bonds":** **I**nflation, **L**iquidity, **C**redit, **M**arket, **P**rice (rate). One letter, one risk.
- **Bond rates inverse rule:** "When rates go UP, bonds bow DOWN." Visualise a see-saw with rates on one side, prices on the other.
- **Risk profiling NAW:** **N**eed, **A**bility, **W**illingness — distributor must balance all three when they conflict.
- **Strategic vs Tactical:** "buffet plan" (strategic — fixed plate sizes) vs "à la carte" (tactical — pick on the fly).
