## How to use this guide

Every chapter has three layers, in this order:

1. **🎯 Summary Card** — what you need to know if you only had 60 seconds.
2. **📖 Core Content** — the testable detail.
3. **🧠 Memory Hooks** — mnemonics and tricks for the parts that don't stick.

Read it twice. Once for understanding (skim Memory Hooks). Second time for retention (memorize them).

## The 4-chapter rule

Master Chapters **4, 5, 9, 12** (50 marks). Even at 70% on these, you're at 35/50. The remaining 8 chapters can be at 60% and you still clear with comfort. Don't try to be perfect everywhere.

## Pass calculus

- Pass mark: 50/100. Realistic target: **65/100** (15-mark cushion).
- Top 4 chapters (50 marks): target 70% = 35
- Mid 4 chapters (30 marks): target 60% = 18
- Bottom 4 chapters (20 marks): target 60% = 12
- **Total: 65/100 ✓**

## What gets tested vs ignored

**Tested heavily:**

- Specific numbers (TER %, holding periods, day counts, percentage limits)
- Exact definitions (large-cap definition, equity-oriented definition)
- Process flows (NFO timeline, KYC steps, redemption settlement)
- Sub-category counts (how many equity sub-categories? **11**)
- Yes/No on permission (can a HUF be a nominee? **No**.)

**Tested lightly:**

- Conceptual essays
- Fund manager strategy nuance
- Anything the workbook hedges with "as decided by SEBI from time to time"
