# Exam Day Checklist

## Before exam day

- [ ] Sleep 7+ hours
- [ ] Re-read this Number Sheet once
- [ ] Skim Chapters 9 and 12 lightly (highest weight)
- [ ] Have a full breakfast

## At the exam centre

- [ ] Carry: government photo ID (Aadhaar + PAN), printed NISM hall ticket, blue/black pen
- [ ] Reach 30 min early
- [ ] Don't carry: smartphone, smartwatch, calculator, written notes (NISM provides if needed)

## During the exam (120 min, 100 questions)

- [ ] First 60 min: answer everything you're confident about; mark uncertain ones
- [ ] Next 30 min: revisit marked questions; eliminate clearly wrong options first
- [ ] Last 30 min: final review; submit all 100 (no negative marking → never leave blank)
- [ ] Quick wins: questions with explicit numbers (TER %, holding period, deadlines) → use Number Sheet recall
- [ ] Tricky: classification questions (which is equity-oriented? which has lock-in?) → think structurally first

## On the morning

Read just the **Number Sheet** + **Common Traps**. Nothing else. Trust the work you've already done.

Good luck.
