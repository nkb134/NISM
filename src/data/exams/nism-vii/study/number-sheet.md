## Margin framework (high-yield)

```
Total margin = VaR + ELM + Adhoc + Exposure + MTM
```

- **VaR**: 99% confidence 1-day worst case.
- **ELM**: tail-risk add-on.
- **Adhoc**: imposed in volatile times.
- **Exposure**: 3-5% of contract value (futures).
- **MTM**: daily P/L true-up.

## Margin shortfall penalty

- **0.5% per day** for first occurrence.
- Escalating for repeats.
- After 5 consecutive instances → broker may need to disable client trading.

## Position-limit reporting alerts

Triggered at **70%, 85%, 100%** of cap.
