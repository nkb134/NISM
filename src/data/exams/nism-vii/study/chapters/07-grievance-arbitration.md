## 🎯 Summary Card

Grievance cascade: **Broker (30 days) → Exchange Investor Service Cell → SEBI SCORES → SMARTODR (online dispute resolution)**. **SMARTODR replaces older exchange-arbitration** for retail. **IPF cap: ₹25 lakh per client per default**. Two-step ODR: **conciliation → arbitration**. Time limits: filing 6 months from cause; arbitration award within 4-5 months. **Limited appeal grounds** under Arbitration Act.

## 📖 Core Content

### Grievance cascade

```
Step 1: Broker compliance officer (30-day window)
   ↓ (if unresolved)
Step 2: Exchange Investor Service Cell (ISC)
   ↓ (if unresolved)
Step 3: SEBI SCORES (online complaint portal)
   ↓ (if unresolved)
Step 4: SMARTODR (Securities Market Approach for Resolution Through ODR)
```

### SCORES (SEBI Complaints Redress System)

- **Online portal** at scores.sebi.gov.in.
- Investor files complaint; broker has timeline to respond.
- SEBI tracks resolution.
- Public dashboard of pending complaints by entity.

### SMARTODR (Online Dispute Resolution)

- Replaces older exchange-arbitration mechanism for retail (effective 2024).
- Two-stage process:
  1. **Conciliation** — neutral conciliator attempts to mediate; parties retain control.
  2. **Arbitration** — if conciliation fails, binding award by arbitrator.
- Conducted entirely online via SEBI-approved ODR institutions.

### Time limits

- **Filing**: within **6 months** of the cause of action (extendable in genuine cases).
- **Conciliation**: 21 days.
- **Arbitration**: ~4-5 months from arbitrator's appointment to final award.
- **Appeal**: limited grounds under Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996; appellate forum available.

### Investor Protection Fund (IPF)

- **Exchange-administered**.
- Compensates clients of a defaulted broker.
- **Cap: ₹25 lakh per client per default**.
- Funded by exchange + member contributions + penalty collections.

### Settlement Guarantee Fund (SGF) vs IPF

| | SGF | IPF |
|---|---|---|
| Administered by | Clearing corp | Exchange |
| Protects | System (settlement integrity) | Investors (against broker default) |
| Cap | None (sized by stress test) | ₹25 lakh per client |

### Common grievance categories

- **Service**: non-receipt of statements, non-issuance of contract notes, wrong charges.
- **Trade disputes**: execution at wrong price, unauthorised orders, late confirmations.
- **Fees**: overcharging brokerage, DP charges, GST.
- **Misrepresentation**: product sold without proper disclosure.
- **Operational**: payout delays, demat-credit issues, KYC lapses.
- **Compliance / ethical**: churning, mis-selling, conflict of interest.

### Investor responsibilities

- **Read contract notes promptly** — flag discrepancies immediately.
- **Maintain records** of orders + statements + correspondence.
- **Verify** broker's ARN + EUIN on AMFI / SEBI sites.
- **Use formal channels** — email + ticket — not just phone calls.
- **File complaint within 6 months** of the cause.

### Investor education + awareness

- **Sahi Hai** AMFI campaign (mutual funds).
- **SEBI Investor Helpline**: toll-free + email + portal.
- **Exchange ISCs** run awareness workshops.
- **Investor Education and Awareness funds** at MFs (≥ 0.02% of TER).

### Broker's reciprocal duties

- **Issue contract note** within 24 hours.
- **Send statement of accounts** quarterly (or monthly if active).
- **Maintain client funds in segregated bank account**.
- **Respond to grievances within 30 days**.
- **Display SCORES + SMARTODR contact** on every page of the broker's site.
- **Annual investor awareness initiatives** as mandated.

### Arbitration award enforcement

- Award is binding and enforceable like a court decree.
- **Limited grounds for appeal** under Section 34 of Arbitration Act:
  - Procedural irregularity
  - Award against public policy
  - Arbitrator beyond authority
- Appeal to High Court or appellate panel constituted by exchange.

### Recent themes (testable)

- **2024 SMARTODR rollout** replacing exchange arbitration for retail.
- **Time-bound settlement** of complaints — penalties on brokers for delay.
- **Cybersecurity grievances** rising — SEBI's CSCRF framework.
- **AI-generated content** disclosure in research / advisory communications.
