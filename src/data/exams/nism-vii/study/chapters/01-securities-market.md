## 🎯 Summary Card

Securities market = **primary** (issuer raises new capital) + **secondary** (existing securities trade). Two depositories: **NSDL (1996), CDSL (1999)**. Three exchanges: **NSE, BSE, MSEI**. Regulators: **SEBI** (capital), **RBI** (banking + G-sec), **IRDAI** (insurance), **PFRDA** (pension). Securities defined in **SCRA Section 2(h)**: shares, debt, derivatives, MF units, REITs/InvITs, G-Secs.

## 📖 Core Content

### Primary vs Secondary

| | Primary | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Funds flow | To issuer | Between investors |
| Mechanism | IPO, FPO, rights, private placement | Cash market, F&O, debt OTC |

### Types of securities

- **Shares** — equity, preference (cumulative/non-cumulative, redeemable/convertible)
- **Debt** — bonds, debentures, NCDs, T-bills, G-secs, CPs
- **Derivatives** — futures, options, swaps
- **Mutual fund units, REITs, InvITs**
- **Government securities, security receipts**

### Indian regulators

| Regulator | Domain |
|---|---|
| **SEBI** | Capital markets, intermediaries, MFs, FPIs |
| **RBI** | Banking, G-secs, currency, money market, payments |
| **IRDAI** | Insurance |
| **PFRDA** | Pension funds (NPS, APY) |
| **MCA** | Companies Act compliance |

### Key intermediaries

- **Stock Exchanges** — NSE, BSE, MSEI
- **Depositories** — NSDL (1996), CDSL (1999)
- **Clearing Corporations** — NCL (NSE), ICCL (BSE), MCCIL (MSEI)
- **Stock Brokers + Authorised Persons** — execute trades for clients
- **RTAs** — maintain investor records (CAMS, KFin)
- **Custodians** — institutional safekeeping
- **Merchant Bankers** — manage IPOs, FPOs, M&A
- **AMCs** — manage MF schemes
- **Investment Advisers + Research Analysts** — separate SEBI registration

### Primary-market routes

- **IPO** — first-time public issue
- **FPO** — already-listed company issues more
- **Rights issue** — to existing shareholders pro-rata
- **Bonus issue** — free shares from reserves
- **Private placement / preferential allotment / QIP** — to selected institutional investors

### Book-building vs Fixed-price

- **Book-building** — price band, investors bid, cut-off determined from demand. Most modern IPOs.
- **Fixed-price** — issuer sets price upfront. Smaller / SME issues.

### Listing rules

Governed by **SEBI ICDR** (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) + **LODR** (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements). Listed companies must publish quarterly results, annual report, material disclosures within prescribed timelines.

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **Primary = funds to issuer; Secondary = funds between investors.**
- **NSDL (1996), CDSL (1999)** — two depositories.
- **SEBI / RBI / IRDAI / PFRDA** = capital / banking / insurance / pension.
- **ICDR governs the issue; LODR governs the listed life.**
- **Book-building > Fixed-price** for most IPOs — price discovered from bids.
- **NCL / ICCL / MCCIL** — clearing corps for NSE / BSE / MSEI.
