# Common Traps for NISM VII

## 1. T+1 vs T+2

> "Cash equity settles on T+2." — **FALSE.** Since **January 2023**, all cash equity settles on **T+1**. T+0 is optional in two windows.

## 2. Sub-broker still exists

> "Sub-brokers are licensed by AMFI." — **FALSE.** Sub-brokers ceased to exist on **April 1, 2019**. Existing ones migrated to **Authorised Person (AP)** status.

## 3. AP can settle clients directly

> "An AP can settle client funds and securities directly." — **FALSE.** AP is the **agent of the TM**; settlement always happens via the TM/CM. AP doesn't hold client money.

## 4. STT applies to all MF redemptions

> "STT applies to debt MF redemptions too." — **FALSE.** STT applies only to **equity-oriented** MF redemptions at 0.001%. Debt MFs have **no STT**.

## 5. CCs and exchanges are the same

> "Clearing happens at the exchange." — **FALSE.** Exchange = trading. **Clearing corp** = post-trade. NCL clears for NSE; ICCL for BSE; MCCIL for MSEI. **Interoperability since 2019** lets you clear at any CC.

## 6. Margin requirement = single number

> "Margin = exchange's published rate." — **FALSE.** Total margin = **VaR + ELM + Adhoc + Exposure + MTM**, dynamically computed. Multiple components stack.

## 7. Pre-trade margin is optional

> "Pre-trade margin is collected only after the trade." — **FALSE.** Mandatory since **September 2020**: collected **before** order placement. Failure = penalty.

## 8. Peak margin is the same as initial margin

> "Peak margin and initial margin are identical." — **FALSE.** Peak = highest intraday position-margin requirement at random sample points; frozen for the day. Initial margin is the static pre-trade requirement.

## 9. Stock F&O settles in cash on expiry

> "Stock futures settle cash on expiry." — **FALSE.** Since **October 2019**, **stock F&O = physical delivery on expiry**. Index F&O remains cash-settled.

## 10. ITM options expire worthless if not exercised

> "ITM options must be exercised by the holder." — **FALSE.** All **ITM options auto-exercised by CC** on expiry. OTM expires worthless.

## 11. Auction = penalty to buyer

> "Auction means the buyer pays extra." — **FALSE.** Auction is triggered when the **seller fails to deliver**; cost goes to **defaulting seller**. Buyer is made whole.

## 12. SGF and IPF are the same

> "SGF protects clients of defaulted brokers." — **FALSE.** **SGF** is at clearing-corp level; protects settlement integrity (system-level). **IPF** is at exchange level; protects clients of defaulted brokers up to **₹25 lakh per client**.

## 13. Contract note timing

> "Contract note can be sent within a week." — **FALSE.** Mandatory **within 24 hours** of trade. Digital DCN allowed.

## 14. Brokerage is the only cost

> "Brokerage is the total trade cost." — **FALSE.** Stack: Brokerage + STT + Stamp duty + Exchange charge + SEBI fee + GST 18% + DP charge (sell from demat). Discount brokers' "₹20 flat" is brokerage only.

## 15. PoA = eDIS

> "PoA and eDIS are interchangeable." — **FALSE.** PoA is the older mechanism (broker-side rights to debit demat). eDIS is **OTP-authenticated, per-transaction** — newer + safer. SEBI nudges toward eDIS.

## 16. NSDL only

> "NSDL is the only depository in India." — **FALSE.** **NSDL (1996) + CDSL (1999)**. Both are SEBI-registered depositories.

## 17. NRE PIS = NRO PIS

> "NRE and NRO PIS accounts behave identically." — **FALSE.** **NRE = full repatriation** (international funds + investments). **NRO = limited / non-repatriable** (India-sourced income).

## 18. MTF is same as F&O

> "MTF and futures both give leverage." — **PARTIALLY.** MTF lets you buy securities **on credit** (broker lends 50% via Group I shares as collateral). Futures = derivative contract, no broker lending. Different products with different rules.

## 19. T+0 is mandatory

> "All cash equity now settles T+0." — **FALSE.** T+0 is **optional, voluntary opt-in**. Default remains T+1. Investor must select T+0 in eligible stocks; two windows (1:30 PM, 3:30 PM).

## 20. Exchange and clearing corp negotiate margins together

> "The exchange sets all margins." — **FALSE.** **Clearing corp** sets margin requirements based on SEBI norms; exchange enforces via the trading platform. CC computes; exchange collects.

## 21. SCORES is the final escalation

> "SCORES is the final step." — **FALSE.** Cascade: Broker → Exchange ISC → SCORES → **SMARTODR**. Since 2024, SMARTODR (Online Dispute Resolution) replaces exchange-arbitration for retail.

## 22. Filing window indefinite

> "Investor can file complaint anytime." — **FALSE.** Filing window: **6 months from cause of action** (extendable in genuine cases).

## 23. Cybersecurity is voluntary

> "Cybersecurity is recommended but not mandated." — **FALSE.** SEBI's **CSCRF** is mandatory; breach reporting within **6 hours**; annual audit; cyber-resilience drills.

## 24. Bulk deal threshold

> "Bulk deals are over 1% of company shares." — **FALSE.** Bulk deal = **> 0.5%** of company shares in a single day. Reported same day. Block deals are negotiated within ±1% of market.

## 25. ASBA without UPI

> "ASBA requires net banking only." — **FALSE.** **UPI mechanism** for ASBA is fully supported for retail (≤ ₹5 lakh). Net banking is the older mechanism.
