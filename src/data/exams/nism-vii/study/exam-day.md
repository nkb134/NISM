# VII Exam Day Checklist

## Night before

- **Re-read [Number Sheet](./number-sheet) + [Memory Hooks](./memory-hooks).** No new material.
- Skip a fresh practice mock.
- Light dinner; 7-8 hours sleep.
- Pack:
  - Photo ID (Aadhaar / PAN / DL — same as registration)
  - Admit card (printout or PDF)
  - Pen (centre may not allow your own — verify)
  - **No** smart watch / mobile / notes — locker provided

## Morning of

- **Arrive 30 min early.** ID + biometric verification takes 10 min.
- Glance at [Number Sheet](./number-sheet) in the lobby.

## During the 120 minutes

### Time strategy

- **120 min for 100 Qs = 72 sec / Q on average.**
- Plan: **40 min for first pass** (~24 sec/Q) — answer obvious.
- **60 min for second pass** on flagged.
- **20 min reserved** for review + toughest 10-15 you skipped.

### Reading discipline

- **Read every option** even when (a) looks right.
- **Watch for "NOT", "EXCEPT", "INCORRECT"** in the stem.
- **Flag-and-skip aggressively** — don't burn 60s on one tough Q while skipping easy ones.

### Negative marking math

| Confidence | Decision |
|---|---|
| You know it | Answer (+1) |
| Narrowed to 2 options | Answer (EV ≈ +0.375) |
| Narrowed to 3 options | Answer (EV ≈ +0.165) |
| 4-option blind | EV ≈ +0.0625 → **skip first pass; revisit at end** |

### Topic-by-topic order

If you can navigate freely (you can on Schoolnet):

1. **Pass 1: Chs 1, 2, 7, 8** — vocabulary + memory + regs, fast.
2. **Pass 2: Chs 4, 5, 6** — risk + clearing + settlement.
3. **Pass 3: Ch 3** — broking ops, the heaviest single chapter.

### Common pitfalls in the moment

- **T+1 vs T+2** — confirm: post-Jan 2023 = T+1.
- **Sub-broker → AP** — never use "sub-broker" in answer.
- **STT rates** — different for delivery / intraday / F&O / options.
- **Stock F&O = physical, Index F&O = cash** since Oct 2019.
- **Auction = seller failed** (cost to seller, not buyer).
- **SGF vs IPF** — system vs investor; don't conflate.

## After

- **Don't compare answers** with others.
- **Result is instant** — Schoolnet shows pass/fail at submit.
- Score sheet emailed within minutes.

## If you fail

- 24-hour cool-off.
- Re-attempt window opens immediately on NISM portal.
- Identify weak topics; redo those chapters + topic drills.

## If you pass

- Certificate available on NISM portal within 30 days.
- **Validity: 3 years.** CPE programme to renew.
- VII is the standard credential for back-office + risk staff at brokers / banks / clearing corps.

---

**Goal: walk out with at least 65/100** — comfortable above the 50/100 pass mark.
