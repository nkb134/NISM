# VII Memory Hooks

## Ch 1 — Securities Market
- **Primary = funds to issuer; Secondary = funds between investors.**
- **NSDL (1996), CDSL (1999)** — two depositories.
- **SEBI / RBI / IRDAI / PFRDA** = capital / banking / insurance / pension.
- **ICDR governs the issue; LODR governs the listed life.**
- **NCL / ICCL / MCCIL** = NSE / BSE / MSEI clearing corps.

## Ch 2 — Market Participants
- **Three blocks: Issuers, Investors, Intermediaries.**
- **Sub-broker → AP April 1, 2019.**
- **Member types**: TM (only trades), TCM (both), SCM (own only), PCM (clears only — bank).
- **FPI Cat I/II/III** — Cat-I lowest scrutiny.
- **IPO allocation: QIB ≥ 50%, NII ≥ 15%, Retail ≥ 35%.** Retail = ≤ ₹2 lakh.
- **AP = agent of TM**; doesn't settle directly.

## Ch 3 — Broking Operations
- **Onboarding 7-step**: KYC → Demat → Bank → Risk → PoA/eDIS → RDD → CMF (UCC).
- **Contract note within 24 hours**; digital DCN allowed.
- **STT delivery 0.1% both sides; intraday 0.025% sell; futures 0.0125% sell; options 0.10% premium sell.**
- **GST 18%** on (brokerage + transaction + SEBI fees).
- **T+1 settlement** for cash equity since Jan 2023.
- **Segregated client funds in separate bank account** — broker can't mingle.
- **Peak margin = intraday max requirement; frozen daily.**
- **Pre-trade margin mandatory since Sep 2020**.
- **UCC = PAN-mapped**; one per PAN per broker.
- **Sub-broker → AP April 2019**.

## Ch 4 — Risk Management
- **Margin stack: VaR + ELM + Adhoc + MTM** (+ Exposure for futures).
- **Pre-trade margin mandatory since Sep 2020.**
- **Peak margin = intraday max, sampled at 4 random points.**
- **At least 50% cash component** for prop margin (T+1).
- **Default cascade: Defaulter → SGF → CC capital → Survivors.**
- **IPF cap = ₹25 lakh per client per default.**
- **Margin shortfall penalty: 0.5%/day** first occurrence; escalates.
- **Cash + BG/FDR + G-Sec = lowest-haircut collateral.**
- **SCORES → SMARTODR** for unresolved client grievance.

## Ch 5 — Clearing
- **Novation** = "CC becomes my counterparty."
- **Three CCs: NCL / ICCL / MCCIL** = NSE / BSE / MSEI.
- **Interoperability 2019** = "trade anywhere, clear anywhere."
- **Multilateral netting** drastically cuts gross obligations.
- **CM net worth: ₹3 cr (others) / ₹1 cr (self).** Plus ₹50 lakh deposit + ₹10 lakh per extra TM.
- **T+1 settlement** since Jan 2023; F&O daily MTM.
- **T+0 optional** since 2024 in two windows (1:30 PM, 3:30 PM).
- **Cross-margin** rewards hedged positions.

## Ch 6 — Settlement
- **T+1 settlement** for cash equity since Jan 2023.
- **T+0 optional** in two windows since 2024.
- **Pay-in ~11 AM, pay-out ~1:30 PM** on T+1.
- **Demat-only since 2019**; no physical certificates.
- **DIS / eDIS** authorise demat debit; eDIS is OTP-based and safer.
- **F&O: stock = physical, index = cash** (since Oct 2019).
- **ITM options auto-exercised** on expiry.
- **Auction = buy-in when seller fails**; cost to defaulter.
- **Ex-date = T-1 of record date** in T+1.
- **Bulk deal = > 0.5% of co shares**; reported same day.

## Ch 7 — Grievance
- **Cascade: Broker (30 days) → Exchange ISC → SCORES → SMARTODR**.
- **SMARTODR replaces old exchange-arbitration** (2024).
- **Conciliation first → Arbitration if failed**.
- **Filing window: 6 months** of cause.
- **IPF cap: ₹25 lakh per client per default**.
- **SGF = settlement integrity; IPF = investor protection**.
- **Award appeal limited grounds** under Arb Act §34.
- **Contract note within 24 hours; statement quarterly**.

## Ch 8 — Other Services
- **MTF: 50% min margin, Group I stocks, separate demat, daily MTM.**
- **NRE PIS = full repat; NRO = limited / non-repat.**
- **ASBA + UPI** for retail IPO; UPI cap ₹5 lakh.
- **Anchor + QIB + NII + Retail** = IPO allocation buckets.
- **RA Regs 2014, IA Regs 2013** = separate registration.
- **Chinese walls** between research + IB + prop trading.
- **CSCRF + DPDPA** = cyber + privacy frameworks; breach report 6 hours.
