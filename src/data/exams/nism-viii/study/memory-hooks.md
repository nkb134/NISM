# Memory Hooks Master List

Every memorisation cue from the chapters, on one page. Use the night before the exam.

## Chapter 1 — Basics

- **FFOS** = Forward, Future, Option, Swap. Forward & Future = obligations both sides; Option = right vs obligation; Swap = stack of forwards.
- **HSA players** = Hedger reduces, Speculator takes, Arbitrageur balances.
- **June 2000 = Indian derivatives birthday.** Index futures first; options came in June + July 2001; stock futures Nov 2001.
- **OTC vs Exchange "C-S-C-C-R"** — Custom vs Standard, Self-margined vs Centrally-margined, Counterparty risk on you vs Clearing corp, Red-tape light vs heavy.
- **Risks "CPLLO"** = Counterparty, Price, Liquidity, Legal, Operational. Plus Basis if hedging.

## Chapter 2 — Index

- **Sensex + Nifty + SX40 = "FFSI"** — Free-Float-Shifted Indices.
- **Impact cost asymmetry rule** — Buy goes UP from ideal, Sell goes DOWN from ideal. Always paid against you.
- **20-45-14** for non-benchmark derivatives — top ≤20%, top 3 ≤45%, ≥14 constituents.
- **Diversification beyond 100 stocks = zero gain.**
- **Weight method by index** — Sensex/Nifty/SX40 = free-float; Dow/Nikkei = price-weighted.

## Chapter 3 — Forwards & Futures

- **Forward → Future** = "from negotiate to standardise."
- **Both buyer and seller pay margin in futures.** Skin in the game on both sides.
- **Basis sign rule** — S > F = +ive basis; F > S = −ive basis (premium).
- **Cost of carry equity = "I minus D"** — Interest minus Dividend.
- **Indian contract value ₹15-20 lakh** since Nov 2024. Was ₹5-10 lakh.
- **NSE Tue, BSE Thu** for expiry.
- **OI ≠ Volume.** OI is the snapshot of outstanding; volume is the flow.
- **Final settlement always at spot.** That's why basis = 0 at expiry.

## Chapter 4 — Options

- **Buyer = Right; Writer = Wrong (obligation).** Always check who has the optionality.
- **All Indian stock/index options = European.** No early-exercise issue.
- **Moneyness "C-Up, P-Down"** — Call ITM when spot is Up above strike; Put ITM when spot is Down below.
- **Intrinsic ≥ 0 always.** Holder won't exercise if intrinsic would be negative.
- **Greeks "DGTVR"** = Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega, Rho. Theta is the silent killer for buyers.
- **BEP rule** — Call BEP = X + P. Put BEP = X − P. Premium INTO the strike, signed by direction.
- **Put-call parity** locks the relative price of same-strike same-expiry European call & put.

## Chapter 5 — Strategies

- **Hedge ratio = "β × Value, over Future × Lot."** Don't forget beta.
- **Cash-and-carry trigger** — F > fair → future is RICH → short it. Reverse if F < fair.
- **Straddle = same strike; Strangle = stretched strikes.** Both need volatility; strangle cheaper, needs bigger move.
- **Covered call CAPS upside; Protective put CAPS downside.** Same word, opposite ends.
- **Collar = both caps stitched together** around a held stock.
- **Long straddle BEPs = X ± total premium.**
- **Delta-hedging** = neutralise direction; gamma forces re-hedging.
- **PCR > 1** = bearish in isolation; contrarian at extremes.

## Chapter 6 — Trading Mechanism

- **TM trades, CM clears, TCM does both, PCM clears only (banks).**
- **Sub-broker → Authorised Person, April 1, 2019.**
- **Order matching = "Price first, Time second."**
- **Stop-loss: trigger between LTP and limit.** Sell SL: trigger > limit.
- **Stock eligibility "1250 / 150 / 50"** — MWPL ≥ ₹1,250 cr; ADTV ≥ ₹150 cr; quarter-sigma ≥ ₹50 lakh. Top 500 universe.
- **Index eligibility "14 / 20 / 45"** — ≥14 stocks; top ≤20%; top-3 ≤45%.
- **Corporate-action adjustment = "ratio of before / after price."**
- **STT side: futures = sell-side; options = sell-premium plus exercised.**

## Chapter 7 — Clearing & Settlement

- **Novation** = Clearing corp becomes legal counterparty to both sides.
- **CM net worth: ₹1 cr (self) / ₹3 cr (others).** Plus ₹50 lakh deposit + ₹10 lakh per extra TM.
- **Interoperability = "trade anywhere, clear anywhere."** One pool of margin.
- **Stock = physical, Index = cash.** Always since Oct 2019.
- **Margin stack = "SPAN + Exposure + ELM + MTM."** SPAN = 99%; ELM = tail.
- **Pay-in / pay-out before market open T+1.**
- **Position-limit cheat sheet** — TM index = max(₹500 cr, 15% OI); Client index = max(₹50 cr, 5% OI).
- **Default cascade: Margin → SGF → CC capital → Survivors.**
- **IPF cap = ₹25 lakh per client.**

## Chapter 8 — Legal & Regulatory

- **SCRA defines, SEBI Act regulates.**
- **Section 18A** — derivatives legal only on a recognised exchange + cleared at recognised CC.
- **Gupta's "50 + 40 + 15-20"** — ≥50 members; ≤40% member rep on governing council; ₹15-20 lakh contract value.
- **Liquid assets ≥ 50% cash component.**
- **Records 5 years; inspections at least yearly.**
- **Cross-default rule** — default in one segment = default in all segments + all exchanges.
- **RDD = signed AT ONBOARDING.**
- **SCORES + SMARTODR** = SEBI's grievance machinery.

## Chapter 9 — Accounting & Taxation

- **Initial margin = current asset, NOT P&L.**
- **Prudence rule = "Show losses, hide gains."** Provide for anticipated loss; ignore anticipated profit.
- **Section 43(5)(d) = derivatives are NON-speculative business income.** THE high-yield tax fact.
- **C/F: Non-spec biz = 8 years; Spec biz = 4 years.**
- **Turnover for derivatives = |P/L| + premium received**, NOT contract value.
- **Buyer pays STT only on exercised options** (0.125% of settlement); else seller side.
- **Stock F&O physical = like cash trade for tax.** Capital gains may apply.

## Chapter 10 — Sales & Investor Protection

- **Sales calls 9 AM – 8 PM** unless asked otherwise.
- **RDD = at onboarding.** Not later, not at first trade.
- **Five red flags "GURI-CHURN"** — Guaranteed returns, Unsuitable recs, Rushed seminars, Impulse trades, CHURNing.
- **Grievance cascade: Broker → SCORES → SMARTODR.**
- **IPF cap: ₹25 lakh per client per default.**
- **PEPs need enhanced due diligence.**
- **STR to FIU-IND within 7 working days.**
- **Three derivative-specific risks**: leverage, margin shortfall, time-decay vs unlimited-loss asymmetry.
- **Counterparty risk = exchange ✗, OTC ✓.**
