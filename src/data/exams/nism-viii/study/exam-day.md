# Exam Day Checklist

## Night before

- **Re-read [Number Sheet](./number-sheet) + [Memory Hooks](./memory-hooks).** That's it. Do NOT cram new material.
- **Do not** take a fresh practice mock — you'll obsess over what you got wrong.
- **Light dinner. 7-8 hours sleep.** Caffeine OK; alcohol not.
- **Pack:**
  - Photo ID (Aadhaar / PAN / DL — same one used at registration)
  - Admit card (printout or PDF on phone)
  - Pen (test centre may not allow your own; check the centre)
  - **No** smart watch, **no** mobile, **no** notes — locker provided
  - Water bottle (allowed in lobby; check inside policy)

## Morning of

- **Arrive 30 min early.** ID + biometric verification takes 10 min; gives buffer for traffic.
- **Glance at the [Number Sheet](./number-sheet)** in the lobby. NOT chapter content.
- **Calm breathing 2 minutes** before the screen unlocks. Adrenaline is the enemy of careful reading.

## During the 120 minutes

### Time strategy

- **120 min for 100 Qs = 72 sec / Q on average.**
- Plan: **40 min for the first pass** at ~24 sec / Q (skim and answer obvious).
- **60 min for the second pass** on flagged questions (numericals, ambiguous wording).
- **20 min reserved** for review + the toughest 10-15 Qs you skipped entirely.

### Reading discipline

- **Read every option.** Even when option (a) looks right, options (c) and (d) might be MORE right.
- **Watch for "NOT", "EXCEPT", "INCORRECT"** in the stem. Highlight in your mind.
- **Flag-and-skip aggressively.** A 30-second tussle on Q 12 that you eventually get wrong costs the same marks as the Q 95 you didn't reach. Move on.

### Negative marking math

| Confidence | Decision |
|---|---|
| **You know it** | Answer. +1 mark. |
| **Narrowed to 2 options** | Answer. EV = 0.5×1 + 0.5×(−0.25) = **+0.375.** |
| **Narrowed to 3 options** | Answer. EV = 0.33×1 + 0.66×(−0.25) ≈ **+0.165.** |
| **No clue (4 options)** | EV = 0.25×1 + 0.75×(−0.25) ≈ **+0.0625.** **Skip on first pass; re-evaluate at the end.** |

### Topic-by-topic order

If the test runner lets you navigate freely (it does on Schoolnet):

1. **Pass 1: Ch 1, 2, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10** — vocabulary + memory questions, fast.
2. **Pass 2: Ch 3, 4** — futures + options, the heavyweights. Worked numericals here.
3. **Pass 3: Ch 5** — strategies, often combine futures + options reasoning. Save for last.

### Common pitfalls in the moment

- **Reading "Long call BEP" vs "Long put BEP"** wrong way. Always rederive: BEP = where total payoff = 0.
- **Confusing exposure margin with initial margin** in a numerical. Initial = SPAN (~99% confidence); Exposure = +3-5% buffer.
- **Forgetting that physical delivery applies to stock F&O** (since Oct 2019). Cash-only intuition will mislead.
- **Misapplying put-call parity** to American options. Indian = European; PCP holds.

## After

- **Don't compare answers** with others outside the centre. You'll second-guess things you got right.
- **Result is instant** — Schoolnet shows pass/fail at submit. Score sheet emailed within minutes.

## If you fail

- **Cool-off: 24 hours.** Do nothing exam-related.
- Re-attempt window opens immediately on NISM portal.
- Most repeaters pass within 2-3 attempts. Identify the 2-3 weakest topics from your score breakdown; redo those chapters + their topic-drill mocks.

## If you pass

- Certificate available on NISM portal within 30 days.
- Validity: **3 years** from passing date. CPE programme to renew.
- Update your LinkedIn — VIII is a recognised credential for derivative dealers, broker employees, traders.

---

**Goal: walk out with at least 70 marks.** That's a clear pass with margin and won't be borderline.
