## 🎯 Summary Card

Three statutes anchor the framework: **SCRA 1956** (defines securities + makes derivatives valid only on a recognised exchange), **SEBI Act 1992** (creates the regulator + powers), **rules and bye-laws of stock exchanges**. Derivative trades are legal **only if** traded on a recognised exchange + cleared through a recognised clearing corp. Membership: net worth norms, KYC strict, **Risk Disclosure Document signed at onboarding**. Default at one segment = default at all segments. Records retained **5 years**.

## 📖 Core Content

### Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956 — SCRA

Defines **securities** to include:
- Shares, scrips, stocks, bonds, debentures, marketable securities of incorporated bodies
- **Derivatives**
- Units of collective investment schemes / mutual funds (excluding ULIPs)
- Government securities, security receipts (under SARFAESI)
- Onshore rupee bonds by ADB, IFC etc.

**Section 18A:** derivatives are legal + valid **only if traded on a recognised exchange + settled at its clearing corp**. OTC equity derivatives between ordinary parties are not enforceable as securities contracts.

### SEBI Act, 1992

Creates SEBI with three statutory mandates:
1. **Protect investors** in securities.
2. **Promote** the development of the securities market.
3. **Regulate** the securities market.

SEBI's powers (high-yield):
- Regulate stock exchanges, intermediaries (brokers, sub-brokers/APs, MFs).
- Prohibit fraudulent + unfair trade practices.
- Inspect, audit, call for information.
- Register self-regulatory organisations.
- Frame regulations for issuance + transfer of securities.

### L. C. Gupta committee (1998) framework

SEBI accepted on **May 11, 1998**. Phased introduction starting with stock index futures (June 2000).

Rules an exchange must satisfy to launch a derivatives segment:
- Separate **governing council**; member representation ≤ **40%**.
- Minimum **50 members**.
- Existing CM-segment members do NOT auto-qualify — must meet Gupta criteria.
- Clearing must go through a SEBI-approved clearing corporation.
- Derivative brokers + dealers + clearing members register **separately** with SEBI (in addition to their CM-segment registration).
- **Minimum contract value ₹15-20 lakh** (since Nov 20, 2024; previously ₹5 lakh from 1998).

### Net worth + collateral norms

| Item | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Clearing member (general) | **₹3 crore** (₹300 lakh) |
| Clearing member (self-only) | ₹1 crore (₹100 lakh) |
| Initial collateral with CC | ₹50 lakh + ₹10 lakh per additional TM cleared |
| Liquid asset split | **≥ 50% cash component**; remainder non-cash (FDR, BG, securities) |

Net worth = Capital + Free reserves *minus* fixed assets, pledged securities, member's card, doubtful debts, prepaid expenses, intangibles, 30% of marketable securities.

### Client onboarding requirements

- **KYC** strict — every client registered with the derivatives broker.
- **Risk Disclosure Document (RDD)** — broker hands over + collects signed copy at onboarding.
- TMs must employ qualified **approved users + sales persons** who passed a SEBI-recognised certification (the exam you're taking right now).
- TMs maintain trade confirmation slips + exercise notices for **5 years**.

### Inspections + enforcement

- All derivatives brokers inspected by the exchange **at least once per year**.
- **Default in one segment = default in all segments + on all exchanges where the member operates** (cross-default).
- Penalties / suspension under SEBI (Stock Broker) Regulations 2026 trigger when:
  - Code of conduct breach
  - Failure to resolve complaints
  - Manipulation, price rigging, cornering
  - Substantial deterioration of financials
  - Fee non-payment
  - Suspension by the exchange

### Position limits — recap

SEBI defines maximum exposures per investor / clearing member / market overall. CM sets sub-limits per TM via the trading system. Once a TM hits limit, only reducing trades allowed; no fresh openers.

### Margin enforcement

- Initial + Exposure margin paid **upfront** by CMs in cash, BG, FDR, T-bills, dated G-secs, or approved demat securities.
- Non-fulfilment of margin = violation; CC may withdraw trading facility, close out positions, invoke BGs / FDRs, levy penalty.
- Liquid assets must continuously cover all margin + net-worth requirements.

### Investor grievance redressal (SEBI's SCORES + ODR)

- **SCORES** (SEBI Complaints Redress System) — online complaint portal; brokers must resolve within prescribed timelines.
- **Online Dispute Resolution (ODR)** — for unresolved disputes, SEBI-approved ODR institutions arbitrate. Replaces the older exchange-level arbitration mechanism for retail.

### Insider trading + UPSI

SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations 2015:
- "Insider" = anyone with access to **Unpublished Price Sensitive Information (UPSI)**.
- Trading in derivatives by an insider in possession of UPSI is barred.
- Trading windows close pre-results; penalties up to **₹25 cr or 3× profits** (whichever higher).

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **SCRA defines, SEBI Act regulates.** SCRA = the rule book for what's a security; SEBI Act = the cop.
- **Section 18A:** "On exchange + on clearing corp = legal." Anywhere else = void.
- **Gupta's "50 + 40 + 15-20":** ≥50 members; ≤40% member rep on governing council; ₹15-20 lakh contract value (since Nov 2024).
- **CM net worth: ₹3 cr (others) / ₹1 cr (self-only).** Plus ₹50 lakh deposit + ₹10 lakh per extra TM.
- **Liquid assets ≥ 50% cash component.** Non-cash gets a haircut.
- **Records kept 5 years.** Inspections at least yearly.
- **Cross-default rule:** "Default in one = default in all segments + all exchanges."
- **Risk Disclosure Document = signed at onboarding.** A trick question on this is almost guaranteed.
- **SCORES + ODR** are SEBI's two-tier grievance machinery.
