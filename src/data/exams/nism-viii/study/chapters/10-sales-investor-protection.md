## 🎯 Summary Card

Brokers owe clients **suitability + KYC + risk disclosure**. Five red flags to spot: **"high return / risk-free", unsuitable recommendations, churning, pushy seminars, unauthorised trades**. **RDD signed at onboarding.** Sales calls only **9 AM – 8 PM** unless customer asks otherwise. **Investor grievance: SCORES → SMARTODR (online dispute resolution)**. **IPF caps payout at ₹25 lakh per client**. Suspicious transactions reported to **FIU-IND** under PMLA. Three risks specific to derivatives: **leverage, margin call inability, options decay (buyer) / unlimited loss (writer)**.

## 📖 Core Content

### Sales-practice obligations

- **Suitability** — recommend only what fits the client's risk profile, horizon, knowledge.
- **No "guaranteed returns"** — flag any pitch promising risk-free or spectacular returns.
- **No churning** — excessive trading just to earn commissions is a violation.
- **No high-pressure tactics, no exploiting inexperience** — particularly with senior citizens.
- **Sales call hours: 9 AM – 8 PM** unless the customer requests otherwise.
- **Identify yourself + employer + purpose** at the start of any sales call.
- **End the call when asked** — voluntarily cease contact if customer says it's inconvenient.
- **Maintain records** of every contact for audit / complaint trail.

### Risk Disclosure Document (RDD)

- Signed by the client **at account opening**, NOT later.
- Lists market risk + liquidity risk + counterparty risk + leverage risk specific to derivatives.
- Emphasises: option buyers can lose 100% of premium quickly; option writers face theoretically unlimited loss.
- **Even after RDD, the broker still owes suitability** — the document is not a get-out-of-jail card.

### Risk profiling — what to capture

| Dimension | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Demographics** | Age, marital status, dependents, education, occupation |
| **Financial** | Personal income, family income, existing liabilities, inherited wealth |
| **Behavioural** | Risk tolerance, investment horizon, prior experience |
| **Goals** | Short-term vs long-term needs, return expectation |

Single-investment-fits-all approach **does NOT work**. Lifecycle approach mandatory.

### KYC + due diligence (anti-money-laundering)

| Stage | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Onboarding** | PAN, Aadhaar, address proof, bank details, photo |
| **PEP screening** | Politically Exposed Persons need **enhanced due diligence** + senior management approval |
| **UCC** | Unique Client Code allotted at onboarding |
| **Periodic re-KYC** | Low-risk: 10 years; medium: 8; high-risk: 2 |
| **Suspicious transactions** | Report to **FIU-IND** under **PMLA 2002**; file STR within 7 working days of detection |

### Investor grievance redressal — the cascade

1. **Complain to the broker first.** Broker has a 30-day response window.
2. If unresolved → **SCORES** (SEBI Complaints Redress System) at scores.sebi.gov.in.
3. **If still unresolved → SMARTODR** (Securities Market Approach for Resolution Through ODR) — SEBI's online dispute resolution platform that replaces older exchange arbitration for retail.
4. ODR institution attempts **conciliation** first; failing that, **arbitration**.
5. Arbitration award binding subject to limited appeal grounds.

### Investor Protection Fund (IPF)

- Exchange-administered safety net for **clients of defaulted brokers**.
- Compensates **legitimate claims up to ₹25 lakh per client per default**.
- Funded by exchange contributions + member contributions + fines.

### Risks specific to derivatives clients (mandatory disclosure)

| Risk | Specific to |
|---|---|
| **Market risk** | All |
| **Liquidity risk** | Especially near expiry, OTM options, illiquid stock contracts |
| **Counterparty risk** | OTC only — exchange F&O has CC guarantee |
| **Leverage risk** | Initial margin tiny vs contract value; small move = big P/L |
| **Margin-call inability** | Daily MTM may force broker to cut position if client can't fund |
| **Sudden margin hike** | In volatility, exchange can raise initial margin same day |
| **Time decay (buyer)** | Options are wasting assets — premium → 0 by expiry |
| **Unlimited loss (writer)** | Option seller's loss is theoretically unbounded |
| **System / glitch risk** | Order may not execute on time during outages |

### Categories of investor complaints (typical)

- **Service** — non-receipt of statements, wrong charges, non-issuance of contract notes.
- **Trade disputes** — execution at wrong price, unauthorised orders, late confirmations.
- **Fees** — overcharging brokerage / DP charges / GST.
- **Misrepresentation** — product sold without proper disclosure.
- **Compliance / ethical** — KYC lapses, churning, mis-selling, payout delays.

### Broker's reciprocal duties

- **Issue contract note** by T+1 with executed prices, charges itemised.
- **Send statement of accounts** quarterly (or monthly if active).
- **Maintain client funds in segregated bank account** — never co-mingled with own / other clients'.
- **Pay-in / pay-out of funds and securities** in line with exchange schedule.
- **Resolve complaints within 30 days** (else escalates to SCORES).

### Clear red flags to watch for

1. **"Guaranteed" / "risk-free" returns** — none exist in markets.
2. Recommendation that doesn't match the client's risk profile (penny stocks to a senior citizen).
3. **Churning** — abnormally high trading frequency in a passive client account.
4. **Investor seminars** pushing rushed decisions on complex products.
5. **Tip-based trading** without research backup.
6. **Front-running / circular trading** — manipulative patterns.

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **Sales calls 9 AM – 8 PM unless asked otherwise.** Stricter than e-commerce hours, on purpose.
- **RDD = signed AT ONBOARDING.** Not later, not on first trade. Highly testable.
- **Five sales-practice red flags = "GURI-CHURN":** **G**uaranteed returns, **U**nsuitable recs, **R**ushed seminars, **I**mpulse trades, **CHURN**ing.
- **Grievance cascade: Broker → SCORES → SMARTODR.** ODR replaces old exchange-arbitration for retail.
- **IPF cap: ₹25 lakh per client per default.** SGF protects the system; IPF protects the investor.
- **PEPs need enhanced due diligence** — senior management approval required on file.
- **STR to FIU-IND within 7 working days** of detecting a suspicious transaction.
- **Three derivative-specific risks:** **leverage, margin shortfall, time decay vs unlimited loss** asymmetry.
- **Counterparty risk = exchange ✗, OTC ✓.** Always check whether trade is on-exchange.
