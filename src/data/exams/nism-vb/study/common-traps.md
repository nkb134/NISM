# Common Traps

## 1. New Cadre vs Full Distributor

> "A V-B distributor can sell mid-cap equity funds." — **FALSE.** New-Cadre distributors can sell only **simple schemes** (Index, Large-cap, Gilt, FMP, equity/gold ETF, Liquid, Ultra-short, Money Market). To sell broader products, pass **NISM-V-A**.

## 2. NAV cut-off for ≥ ₹2 lakh subscriptions

> Subscriptions of ₹2 lakh or more get **NAV based on the date funds are realised by the AMC**, not the application timestamp. Even if you submit the application at 2:50 PM, if funds clear next day, that day's NAV applies.

## 3. Liquid fund cut-off is DIFFERENT

> Subscription cut-off for **liquid funds is 1:30 PM**, not 3:00 PM. Redemption cut-off is 3:00 PM (same as other schemes).

## 4. Equity tax rates updated July 2024

> Pre-July 2024: STCG 15%, LTCG 10% beyond ₹1 lakh.
> Post-July 2024: **STCG 20%, LTCG 12.5% beyond ₹1.25 lakh**.
> Old questions still test old rates — be sure which version of the syllabus you're answering.

## 5. Debt fund tax — April 2023 cliff

> Debt funds **bought on or after April 1, 2023** are taxed at **slab rate, regardless of holding period** — no indexation, no LTCG concession.
> Debt funds bought BEFORE April 1, 2023 follow old rules: STCG slab; LTCG 20% with indexation after 36 months.

## 6. DDT abolished, dividends now slab-taxed

> Pre-FY21: Dividends were tax-free in investor's hands (DDT paid by AMC).
> Post-FY21: **DDT abolished**. Dividends taxed at **investor's slab rate**. **TDS 10%** if dividend > ₹5,000 per FY.

## 7. ELSS lock-in is 3 years

> Common confusion: PPF is 15 years; tax-saving FD is 5 years; **ELSS is just 3 years** — shortest among Section 80C instruments. After lock-in, treated like any equity scheme.

## 8. SIP installments have separate holding periods

> Each SIP installment has its OWN holding period for capital gains. So in a 5-year SIP, the most recent installments are STCG while older ones are LTCG. Common surprise for first redemption.

## 9. Switch / STP triggers tax

> A switch (or STP installment) is treated as a **redemption + purchase** for tax. STCG/LTCG applies on the source-scheme leg. Common mis-understanding — investors often think it's tax-neutral.

## 10. Direct vs Regular plan = same scheme, different cost

> Direct plan and Regular plan are the SAME scheme — same portfolio, same fund manager, same returns before fees. **Only TER differs.** Direct plan has no distributor commission baked in → ~0.5-1.0% lower TER → materially higher long-run returns.

## 11. Three documents — SID vs SAI vs KIM

- **SID** = scheme detail (legally binding)
- **SAI** = AMC + institutional context
- **KIM** = abridged 1-2 page summary (handed with form)

KIM is the FIRST read for the investor. SID is the legally binding detail.

## 12. NAV calculation — daily mandatory

> NAV is calculated **daily** for open-ended schemes; **periodically** for close-ended (typically weekly). Disclosed by 11 PM on AMC + AMFI sites.

## 13. AMFI Code — no rebating, no inducement

> Distributors **cannot pass back commissions** to investors (no rebating). Cannot accept gifts above **₹500** from AMCs. Both are explicit AMFI Code violations.

## 14. Trail commission only since 2018

> SEBI banned upfront commissions in 2018 (with limited exceptions for B-30 first-time investors). Distributors now earn **trail commissions only** — annual % of AUM as long as the investor stays invested.

## 15. KYC re-verification frequency

- Low risk: **10 years**
- Medium risk: **8 years**
- High risk: **2 years**

Common trap: candidates flip the order.

## 16. STR vs CTR

- **STR (Suspicious Transaction Report)** = unusual patterns, regardless of value. Filed within **7 working days** of detection.
- **CTR (Cash Transaction Report)** = cash transaction ≥ **₹10 lakh** (or aggregate in a month). Filed monthly.

## 17. Open offer / SAST is for stocks, not MF units

> Don't confuse the 25% open offer rule (SAST) with MF-specific concepts. SAST applies to acquiring stakes in listed companies. MFs don't have open offers — investors are unitholders, not shareholders of operating companies.

## 18. AMC min net worth = ₹50 cr (not ₹50 lakh)

> AMC = ₹50 **crore** net worth. Don't confuse with the ₹50 **lakh** minimum sponsor commitment to scheme.

## 19. Sponsor contribution to AMC = 40%, not majority

> Sponsor must contribute **min 40%** to AMC's net worth — NOT 51% (majority). Less than 40% disqualifies the sponsor relationship.

## 20. Up to 3 nominees per folio

> SEBI allows **up to 3 nominees** with percentages summing to 100%. Older info often says "1 nominee only" — outdated. Investor must opt-out explicitly if not nominating.

## 21. Index funds vs ETFs — demat requirement

| | Index fund | ETF |
|---|---|---|
| Demat needed | No | Yes |
| Trading | Off-exchange (AMC at NAV) | On-exchange (live during trading hours) |

## 22. Ad code: performance ads need >1 year track record

> Schemes < 1 year cannot show performance in ads. Between 1-3 yrs: annualised yields. > 3 yrs: **CAGR for 1, 3, 5 years and since inception** is mandatory.

## 23. Investor education = 0.02% of TER (not 1%)

> AMCs allocate **at least 0.02% of the scheme's TER** for investor education — NOT 1%, NOT 0.2%. Common decimal confusion.

## 24. Negative marking? No.

> V-B has **no negative marking**. Different from VIII/XV which have 25% penalty. Strategy: **answer EVERY question**, even blind guesses.

## 25. ARN vs EUIN

- **ARN (AMFI Registration Number)** = the entity / firm registration.
- **EUIN (Employee Unique Identification Number)** = the advising employee.
- Both must appear on the application form when advice is given.
