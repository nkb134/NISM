# V-B Memory Hooks

## Ch 1 — Investment Landscape
- **SLR for evaluation:** **S**afety, **L**iquidity, **R**eturns.
- **Five risks "ILCMP":** Inflation, Liquidity, Credit, Market, Interest rate.
- **Bond rates inverse rule:** "rates UP, bond prices DOWN."
- **NAW for risk profile:** **N**eed, **A**bility, **W**illingness.
- **Strategic vs Tactical** = "fixed plate" vs "à la carte."

## Ch 2 — Concept & Role of MF
- **MF benefits "DPL + RTC"** — Diversification, Professional management, Liquidity + Regulatory protection, Tax efficiency, Convenience.
- **Sponsor → Trust → AMC** structure (next chapter).
- **Open-ended = continuous; Close-ended = fixed tenor + exchange-listed.**
- **NAV = Net assets / Units.** Daily for open-ended.
- **TER falls as AUM grows** — economies of scale baked in.
- **SIP / STP / SWP** = the three systematic plans.

## Ch 3 — Legal Structure
- **Three-tier: Sponsor → Trust (Trustees) → AMC.**
- **Sponsor min 40%** of AMC net worth + 5-year track record.
- **Min 4 trustees; ≥ 2/3 independent.**
- **AMC min net worth ₹50 crore.**
- **Custodian + RTA are independent of AMC.**
- **AMC skin-in-the-game: min 1% of scheme corpus or ₹50 lakh.**
- **CAMS + KFin** = the two main RTAs.

## Ch 4 — MF Products (New Cadre)
- **New Cadre = simple, transparent products only.**
- **CAN sell: Index, Large-cap, Gilt, FMP, equity/gold ETF, Liquid, MM.**
- **CANNOT sell: Mid/Small-cap, Sectoral, Credit-risk, Aggressive Hybrid, International, Derivatives.**
- **To sell broader products → pass NISM-V-A.**
- **Index fund vs ETF:** off-exchange NAV vs on-exchange live; no demat vs demat needed.

## Ch 5 — Scheme Information
- **Three docs: SID + SAI + KIM.** SID = scheme detail; SAI = institutional; KIM = abridged.
- **Riskometer: 6 levels** (Low → Very High); updated **monthly**.
- **NFO max 15 days; reopen within 5 business days** for open-ended.
- **Cut-off: 3 PM** for non-liquid; **1:30 PM subscription** for liquid.
- **Subscription ≥ ₹2 lakh** = NAV based on funds-realised date.
- **Direct plan = lower TER** by 0.5-1.0% than Regular.
- **Monthly factsheet** is the recurring disclosure.

## Ch 6 — Distribution & Channel
- **MFD = NISM V-A; New Cadre = V-B; RIA = X-A.**
- **ARN is for the entity; EUIN is for the advising employee.**
- **Trail commission only since 2018** — no upfront (except B-30).
- **Direct plan = lower TER; Regular plan has trail bundled in.**
- **AMFI Code: disclose, no rebate, no inducement, confidentiality, no mis-sell.**
- **Gifts from AMC to distributor capped at ₹500.**
- **CKYC = one-time KYC across the industry.**
- **AMFI publishes annual distributor commission report.**

## Ch 7 — Performance
- **CAGR for ≥1 yr; absolute for <1 yr; XIRR for SIP.**
- **Rolling returns > point-to-point** for honest comparison.
- **Alpha > 0 = outperformance** vs benchmark.
- **Sharpe = total risk; Treynor = systematic; Sortino = downside only.**
- **Tracking error LOWER = better** for index funds.
- **β = 1 moves with benchmark; > 1 more volatile.**
- **Past performance is NOT a guarantee** — SEBI mandate.

## Ch 8 — Taxation
- **Equity STCG = 20% (post-July 2024); Equity LTCG = 12.5% above ₹1.25 lakh** per FY.
- **Debt bought after April 1, 2023 = slab rate, no indexation, no LTCG benefit.**
- **DDT abolished FY 21**; dividends taxed at slab.
- **TDS on dividend > ₹5,000 per FY = 10%.**
- **STT on equity MF redemption = 0.001%; no STT on debt MF.**
- **Switch / STP = redemption + purchase** for tax. Triggers gains.
- **ELSS = only Section 80C MF**; 3-year lock-in.
- **STCG loss can offset any cap gain; LTCG loss only LTCG. Both carry 8 years.**

## Ch 9 — Investor Services
- **CKYC = one-time across the industry.**
- **PAN + Aadhaar + bank cheque + photo** = standard KYC docs.
- **Re-KYC: 10 yrs (low risk) / 8 / 2 (high).**
- **NAV cut-off: 3 PM** (other than liquid).
- **Subscription ≥ ₹2 lakh → NAV based on funds-realised date.**
- **Up to 3 nominees** per folio; percentages sum to 100%.
- **Redemption payout T+2 equity; T+1 liquid.**
- **CAS = AMFI's consolidated account statement** across all AMCs.
- **Unclaimed > 3 years → paid at 3-year-end NAV** (not current).
- **Grievance cascade: AMC → AMFI → SCORES → SMARTODR.**
- **Investor awareness: ≥ 0.02% of TER** allocated by AMCs.
- **"Mutual Funds Sahi Hai"** = AMFI's awareness campaign.

## Ch 10 — Legal & Regulatory
- **SEBI Act 1992 + SEBI (MF) Regulations 2026** = the regulatory anchor.
- **AMFI = SRO + ARN issuer + investor awareness.**
- **ELSS must hold ≥ 80% equity** for §80C eligibility.
- **Ads: > 1 year old; > 3 yr → CAGR 1/3/5/inception mandatory.**
- **Celebrity endorsements: industry-level only**, with SEBI approval.
- **AML: STR to FIU-IND within 7 working days; CTR for cash ≥ ₹10 lakh.**
- **Records retained 5 years.**
- **Penalty cascade: warning → fine → suspension → cancellation → prosecution.**
- **Finfluencer rules (2024)** require SEBI registration to comment on specific securities/investments.
