## 🎯 Summary Card

Two anchors: **SEBI Act 1992** + **SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations 2026** (replacing the older 1996 framework). SEBI regulates AMCs, schemes, and distributors. **AMFI** is the SRO for the MF industry; sets a Code of Conduct + investor education + best practices. Distributors must comply with **AMFI Code of Conduct + AML/PMLA** (suspicious-transaction reporting). Penalties for mis-selling, unregistered selling, KYC violations, rebating.

## 📖 Core Content

### SEBI Act 1992

Establishes SEBI as the regulator. Powers:
1. **Protect investors** in securities.
2. **Promote** the development of the securities market.
3. **Regulate** the securities market.

Specific powers over MFs:
- Register + regulate AMCs, trustees, mutual funds.
- Frame the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations.
- Inspect, audit, call for information.
- Penalty + suspension.

### SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations 2026

The current rulebook (replacing the 1996 regs framed earlier).

Key areas:
- **Registration of MFs + AMCs**
- **Disclosures** (SID/SAI/KIM/portfolio/NAV/risk)
- **Categorisation** (2017 framework)
- **Mergers + conversions**
- **Risk management** (concentration limits, exposure limits)
- **Investment restrictions** (per-issuer, per-sector, per-instrument caps)
- **Advertisement code**
- **Grievance redressal**
- **Corporate debt market** participation by MFs

### Investment limits (recap from V-A)

| Limit | Rule |
|---|---|
| Single company equity (per scheme) | Max **10%** of NAV |
| MF (across all schemes) ownership of single co | Max **10%** of voting rights |
| Single company debt (per scheme) | Max **10%** of NAV, extendable to **12%** with Trustee + AMC Board approval |
| Sponsor associates equity (across MF) | Max **25%** of net assets |
| Single bank exposure (per scheme) | Max **15%** of NAV, extendable to **20%** |
| Unrated debt (per scheme) | Max **10%** of debt portfolio, min investment grade |

> **ELSS** must hold min **80% in equity** to qualify for the §80C tax benefit (a scheme-attribute, not an exposure limit).

### Advertisement code (high-yield)

- **Performance ads** only if scheme is **>1 year old**.
- For schemes 1-3 years old: annualised yields if performance available for ≥7, 15, or 30 days.
- For schemes >3 years: **CAGR for 1, 3, 5 yrs and since inception** (mandatory).
- **Debt benchmarks** = 10-yr G-sec or relevant CRISIL index.
- **Celebrity endorsements**: industry-level only (not scheme-specific). Prior SEBI approval.

### AMFI (Association of Mutual Funds in India)

- Industry association formed 1995.
- Acts as a self-regulatory organisation.
- Sets Code of Conduct for distributors.
- Issues ARN to distributors.
- Conducts investor awareness ("Mutual Funds Sahi Hai").
- Publishes monthly industry data.

### AMFI Code of Conduct (distributor)

- **Full disclosure** of commission + conflicts.
- **No rebating** (passing back commission).
- **No inducement** via gifts (cap ₹500 from AMC).
- **Confidentiality** of investor data.
- **KYC compliance** for every investor.
- **Avoid mis-selling** — recommend only what fits.
- **No splitting** of applications to game commission.
- **Grievance handling** within 30 days.

### Anti-Money Laundering (PMLA 2002)

Distributors are reporting entities under PMLA. Must:
- Identify clients (KYC + CKYC).
- Maintain transaction records for **5 years**.
- Report **Suspicious Transactions (STR)** to **FIU-IND** within **7 working days** of detection.
- Report **Cash Transactions Reports (CTR)** for cash transactions ≥ ₹10 lakh / aggregate ≥ ₹10 lakh in a month.
- **Internal SOPs** + designated **Principal Officer** for AML compliance.

### Suspicious-transaction red flags

- Large cash transactions inconsistent with investor profile.
- Investments split into amounts just below reporting threshold.
- Investor reluctant to provide KYC information.
- Unusual transaction patterns (rapid in-out across schemes).
- Investor of unclear source of funds.
- Cross-border activity inconsistent with declared profile.

### Investor grievance redressal — regulatory layer

| Layer | Window |
|---|---|
| AMC's investor service team | 30 days |
| AMC's compliance officer | Escalation |
| AMFI | Industry-level escalation |
| **SEBI's SCORES** | Online portal — final regulatory recourse |
| **SMARTODR** | Online dispute resolution platform (replaces older arbitration) |

### Penalties for distributor mis-conduct

- **Warning** → **Monetary penalty** → **ARN suspension** → **ARN cancellation** → **Criminal prosecution** for serious cases (fraud, mis-appropriation).

### Privacy + data protection

- **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023** — distributors are "data fiduciaries" handling investor PII.
- **Consent-based collection**, **purpose limitation**, **retention** as per regulatory norms (5 years for KYC + transaction records).
- Investor can request data deletion (subject to regulatory retention obligations).

### Recent themes (typical updates tested)

- **Risk-o-meter** revisions (post-2021 model overhaul).
- **Direct plan disclosure** mandates.
- **TER tightening** for specific scheme categories.
- **Influencer / finfluencer** rules: SEBI's 2024 guidelines requiring SEBI-registered RIAs / RAs / SEBI-registered intermediaries to comment on investments.

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **SEBI Act 1992 + SEBI (MF) Regulations 2026** = the regulatory anchor.
- **AMFI = SRO + ARN issuer + investor awareness.**
- **ELSS must hold ≥ 80% equity** for §80C eligibility.
- **Ads: > 1 year old; > 3 yr → CAGR 1/3/5/inception mandatory.**
- **Celebrity endorsements: industry-level only**, with SEBI approval.
- **AML: STR to FIU-IND within 7 working days; CTR for cash ≥ ₹10 lakh.**
- **Records retained 5 years.**
- **Penalty cascade: warning → fine → suspension → cancellation → prosecution.**
- **DPDPA 2023** brings consent + purpose-limitation rules to investor PII.
- **Finfluencer rules (2024)** require SEBI registration to comment on specific securities/investments.
