## 🎯 Summary Card

MF distributors are SEBI/AMFI-registered intermediaries who help investors choose + transact in schemes. Two registration regimes: **MFD (NISM V-A) + RIA (NISM X-A)**. Distributors paid through **trail commission** (annual % of AUM, throughout investment period) — not upfront. **AMFI Code of Conduct** + EUIN + ARN rules apply. Direct vs Regular Plans = lever for cost-conscious investors.

## 📖 Core Content

### Distributor categories

| Category | Mandatory cert | Can sell |
|---|---|---|
| **Mutual Fund Distributor (MFD)** | NISM V-A | All mutual fund schemes |
| **New Cadre Distributor** | NISM V-B | Limited list (Index, Gilt, Liquid, etc.) |
| **Registered Investment Adviser (RIA)** | NISM X-A | Advise + transact, fee-only model |
| **Bank-based distribution** | NISM V-A (employees) | All schemes; channel of bank |

### ARN (AMFI Registration Number)

Every distributor must have a **valid ARN** from AMFI to deal in MFs.

- Issued after passing the relevant NISM exam.
- Valid 3 years; renew via CPE programme + re-pass before expiry.
- Disclosure: ARN must appear on every advice + transaction note + invoice.

### EUIN (Employee Unique Identification Number)

- For employees (RM/IFA staff) of an MFD/IFA who actually advise.
- One EUIN per advising employee, ARN per organisation.
- Mandatory disclosure on the application form when advice is given.

### Commission structure

- **Trail commission only** — annual % of AUM (typically 0.5-1% for equity, 0.05-0.5% for debt). Paid as long as the investor stays invested.
- **No upfront commission** since 2018 SEBI ban (with a few specified exceptions for B-30 cities).
- **B-30 special incentive** — for the first time investor from cities beyond top 30, additional one-time commission allowed (was 1% earlier; revised periodically).
- **Direct plan investors** pay no commission → lower TER → higher net returns.

### AMFI Code of Conduct (high-yield)

Distributors must:
- **Full disclosure** of commissions (in transaction note + on the AMFI website).
- **No rebating** — passing back commission to investors is prohibited.
- **No inducement** via gifts or extraneous benefits (ban on gifts > ₹500 from AMC to distributor).
- **Confidentiality** of investor data.
- **Disclose conflicts** of interest.
- **KYC compliance** for every investor.
- **Avoid mis-selling** — recommend products fitting risk profile + horizon.
- **No splitting** — single application not split to game commission.
- **Distributor leaving** — investor must be informed; folio handled per investor instruction.

### Mis-selling indicators

- Selling **high-commission products** when low-commission ones fit better.
- **Switching** investor between schemes purely for commission.
- **Hiding risks** of higher-risk schemes.
- **Inducing** based on past returns alone.
- **Pressure tactics** to buy now / before NFO closes.

### Direct vs Regular — the founder's lens

| | Regular Plan | Direct Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Distributor involved | Yes — advises + earns trail | No — investor goes direct to AMC |
| TER | Higher (by ~0.5-1.0%) | Lower |
| Long-run returns | Lower | Higher (by the TER differential, compounded) |
| Best for | Investors needing advice / hand-holding | Self-directed, financially literate investors |

### Investor onboarding flow (with distributor)

1. **KYC** — PAN, Aadhaar, address proof, photo. One-time, accepted across the industry (CKYC).
2. **Risk profiling** — questionnaire to gauge risk tolerance.
3. **Goal mapping** — short-term, mid-term, long-term goals.
4. **Scheme recommendation** based on risk + horizon + need.
5. **Application** — physical form or online (RTA portal, AMC site, MF Utility, distributor app).
6. **Funding** — UPI/NEFT/cheque to the AMC's account.
7. **Confirmation** — units credited within T+1 (T+2 for international).

### Investor servicing post-investment

- **SoA (Statement of Account)** sent monthly via email.
- **Transaction confirmation** within 5 working days.
- **Folio servicing** — change of bank, nominee, address.
- **CAMS / KFin online portals** for self-service.

### AMFI website + transparency

- AMFI publishes **distributor commissions disclosure** annually — top distributors + their total commissions.
- Investors can verify the distributor's ARN + EUIN on AMFI website.
- Distributor performance visible — helps investors compare.

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **MFD = NISM V-A; New Cadre = V-B; RIA = X-A.**
- **ARN is for the entity; EUIN is for the advising employee.**
- **Trail commission only since 2018** — no upfront (except B-30).
- **Direct plan = lower TER; Regular plan has trail bundled in.**
- **AMFI Code: disclose, no rebate, no inducement, confidentiality, no mis-sell.**
- **Gifts from AMC to distributor capped at ₹500.**
- **CKYC = one-time KYC across the industry.**
- **AMFI publishes annual distributor commission report.**
