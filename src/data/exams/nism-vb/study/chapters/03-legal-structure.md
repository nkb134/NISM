## 🎯 Summary Card

Indian MFs are **trusts** under the **Indian Trusts Act 1882**. Three-tier structure: **Sponsor → Trustees → AMC**. Sponsor sets up the trust + contributes minimum capital. Trustees safeguard investor interest. AMC manages day-to-day operations. **Custodian** holds securities; **RTA** maintains investor records. SEBI regulates the entire chain via the **SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations 2026**.

## 📖 Core Content

### Three-tier structure

```
Sponsor   →   Trust   →   AMC
              (Trustees)
```

| Entity | Role |
|---|---|
| **Sponsor** | Establishes the MF; contributes minimum capital; selects trustees |
| **Trustees** | Safeguard investor interest; appoint/oversee AMC; minimum 4 trustees, 2/3 independent |
| **AMC (Asset Management Company)** | Day-to-day operations: investment decisions, marketing, customer service |
| **Custodian** | Holds the MF's securities in safekeeping |
| **RTA (Registrar & Transfer Agent)** | Maintains investor records, processes transactions |

### Sponsor eligibility

- Sound track record (minimum 5 years in financial services).
- Net worth: positive in each of the preceding 5 years.
- Profitability in 3 of last 5 years.
- **Minimum sponsor contribution: 40%** to the AMC's net worth.

### Trustees

- **Minimum 4 trustees**; **at least 2/3 independent** of the Sponsor.
- Trustees act in fiduciary capacity for investors.
- Approve major decisions (NFOs, mergers, fee changes).
- Quarterly compliance reports to SEBI.

### AMC

- Public limited company registered with SEBI.
- **Minimum net worth: ₹50 crore.**
- **Sponsor stake in AMC: minimum 40%.**
- Must have a **full-time CEO + CIO + Compliance Officer + Risk Officer**.
- Cannot undertake any other business without SEBI approval.

### Custodian

- Independent entity (not part of AMC).
- SEBI-registered.
- Holds securities in dematerialised form.
- Provides reporting + corporate-action handling.

### RTA

- Independent or AMC-owned.
- Common RTAs in India: CAMS, KFin Technologies.
- Maintains folios, processes purchases / redemptions, calculates NAV-based units.

### Investor protection mechanisms

- **Trust structure** — assets segregated from sponsor / AMC.
- **Custodian** — independent safekeeping.
- **Auditor** — independent audit of MF accounts.
- **SEBI** — regulator with inspection + enforcement powers.
- **AMFI** — self-regulatory body for MF industry.
- **Investor Education and Awareness initiatives** — funded from MF expense ratio.

### Sponsor + AMC contribution to schemes

SEBI mandates that the AMC (or its associates) contribute at least **1% of the amount raised, subject to a maximum of ₹50 lakh** in every scheme — to ensure skin-in-the-game.

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **Three-tier: Sponsor → Trust (Trustees) → AMC.**
- **Sponsor contributes min 40% of AMC net worth + has 5-year track record.**
- **Min 4 trustees; ≥ 2/3 independent.**
- **AMC min net worth ₹50 crore.**
- **Custodian + RTA are independent of AMC.**
- **AMC skin-in-the-game: min 1% of scheme corpus or ₹50 lakh.**
- **CAMS + KFin** = the two main RTAs in India.
