## 🎯 Summary Card

Saving precedes investing. Evaluate any investment by **Safety, Liquidity, Returns**. **Real return = Nominal − Inflation** (can be negative). Five investment risks: **inflation, liquidity, credit, market/price, interest rate**. Diversification kills company-specific risk only. Asset allocation: **strategic** (target %, rebalance) or **tactical** (active shifts).

## 📖 Core Content

### Saving vs Investment

- **Saving** = setting aside money by reducing consumption.
- **Investment** = deploying saved money to earn returns.

### Three factors to evaluate any investment

**Safety, Liquidity, Returns** + convenience, ticket size, taxability.

Returns = Current income (interest, dividend, rent) + Capital appreciation (only on sale).

**Real return = Nominal − Inflation.** If FD pays 7% and inflation is 8%, real return is **negative**.

### Asset classes (foundational)

- **Equity** — ownership in a company; high return, high risk.
- **Fixed income** — bonds, FDs, debentures; predictable interest.
- **Real estate** — property + REITs/InvITs.
- **Commodities** — gold, silver.
- **Hybrid** — combinations (e.g. balanced funds).

### Five investment risks

1. **Inflation risk** — purchasing power erodes.
2. **Liquidity risk** — can't convert to cash quickly.
3. **Credit risk** — issuer defaults; G-secs lowest in country.
4. **Market / Price risk** — broad-market and company-specific. Diversification handles only the latter.
5. **Interest rate risk** — bond prices move INVERSELY to rates.

### Risk profiling

- **Need** to take risk (do goals require it?)
- **Ability** (capacity, horizon)
- **Willingness** (psychological tolerance)

Distributor must balance all three.

### Asset allocation

- **Strategic** — target % allocation; rebalance on drift.
- **Tactical** — active shifts to exploit opportunities; for sophisticated investors.

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **SLR for evaluation:** **S**afety, **L**iquidity, **R**eturns.
- **Five risks "ILCMP":** Inflation, Liquidity, Credit, Market, Interest rate.
- **Bond rates inverse rule:** "rates UP, bond prices DOWN."
- **NAW for risk profile:** **N**eed, **A**bility, **W**illingness.
- **Strategic vs Tactical** = "fixed plate" vs "à la carte."
