## 🎯 Summary Card

Every MF scheme is governed by three documents: **SID (Scheme Information Document)**, **SAI (Statement of Additional Information)**, and **KIM (Key Information Memorandum)**. **Riskometer** rates scheme risk on 6 levels (Low → Very High). **NFO (New Fund Offer)** runs max 15 days. After NFO, open-ended schemes reopen for continuous subscription/redemption within 5 business days.

## 📖 Core Content

### The three scheme documents

| Document | What it contains | Mandatory frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **SID (Scheme Information Document)** | Scheme objective, investment strategy, asset allocation, benchmark, fund manager, expenses, risk factors, taxation | Every NFO + at least annual update |
| **SAI (Statement of Additional Information)** | Information about the AMC, sponsors, trustees, custodian, fees, valuation policies, common to multiple schemes | Annual update |
| **KIM (Key Information Memorandum)** | Abridged 1-2 page summary of SID + SAI; given with every application form | Updated whenever SID changes |

> **Investor's first read = KIM.** SID is the legally binding detail; SAI is the institutional context.

### Riskometer (mandatory disclosure)

SEBI mandates a **6-level riskometer** for every scheme:

```
Low → Low to Moderate → Moderate → Moderately High → High → Very High
```

- Updated **monthly** by the AMC.
- Calculated using a **risk score** from quantitative inputs (volatility, credit quality, liquidity, etc.).
- Past changes in riskometer must be disclosed.

### NFO (New Fund Offer)

- **Maximum NFO period: 15 days.**
- Open-ended schemes reopen for continuous subscription/redemption within **5 business days** of NFO close.
- Close-ended schemes don't reopen — only mature.
- Allotment of units must happen within 5 business days of NFO close.

### Cut-off times (high-yield)

| Scheme type | Subscription cut-off | Redemption cut-off |
|---|---|---|
| **Liquid** | 1:30 PM | 3:00 PM |
| **Other than liquid** | 3:00 PM | 3:00 PM |

If application + funds received by cut-off → that day's NAV. After cut-off → next day's NAV. **For purchases ≥ ₹2 lakh, NAV is applicable only when funds are credited to AMC's account** — not just the application timestamp.

### NAV applicability in detail

- **Subscription < ₹2 lakh, application before 3 PM** → same-day NAV.
- **Subscription ≥ ₹2 lakh** → NAV of the day funds are realised by AMC.
- **Liquid funds:** previous day's NAV applicable if subscription before 1:30 PM (carry-back rule for next-day allotment).
- **Redemption applied before 3 PM** → same-day NAV.

### Benchmark assignment

Every scheme must specify a **benchmark index** in its SID. Performance vs benchmark must be disclosed:
- Equity Large Cap → Nifty 100
- Mid Cap → Nifty Midcap 150
- Liquid → CRISIL Liquid Fund Index
- Gilt → CRISIL Dynamic Gilt Index
- Hybrid → CRISIL Hybrid Index series

### Fund manager disclosure

- Name + experience disclosed in SID + monthly factsheet.
- Tenure on the scheme + total industry experience.
- Investor can track fund-manager changes via continuous disclosure.

### Monthly factsheet

Mandatory disclosure document:
- Portfolio composition (top 10 holdings, sector weights)
- Performance vs benchmark
- AUM
- Fund manager details
- Risk metrics (β, σ, Sharpe, R²)
- Expense ratio breakdown

### Direct vs Regular plan

Every open-ended scheme must offer both:
- **Regular Plan** — distributor commission embedded in TER → higher TER.
- **Direct Plan** — no distributor commission → **lower TER** by ~0.5-1.0%.
- Compounded over years, Direct Plans deliver materially higher returns.
- Same scheme; same portfolio; only the cost structure differs.

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **Three docs: SID + SAI + KIM.** SID = scheme detail; SAI = institutional; KIM = abridged.
- **Riskometer: 6 levels** (Low → Very High); updated **monthly**.
- **NFO max 15 days; reopen within 5 business days** for open-ended.
- **Cut-off: 3 PM** for non-liquid; **1:30 PM subscription** for liquid.
- **Subscription ≥ ₹2 lakh** = NAV based on funds-realised date.
- **Direct plan = lower TER** by 0.5-1.0% than Regular.
- **Monthly factsheet** is the recurring disclosure.
