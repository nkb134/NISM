## 🎯 Summary Card

A **mutual fund (MF)** pools money from investors and invests in securities professionally. Three benefits: **diversification, professional management, liquidity**. Two structural types: **open-ended** (continuous subscription/redemption) and **close-ended** (fixed tenor). Schemes categorised by **asset class** (equity, debt, hybrid, solution-oriented) and **strategy** (active vs passive). NAV = Net Assets / Units. **Total Expense Ratio (TER)** capped by SEBI.

## 📖 Core Content

### What a mutual fund is

A **trust** that pools investor money and invests via the **AMC (Asset Management Company)** in equities, bonds, and other securities. Investors hold **units**, not direct securities. **Sponsor → Trust → AMC** is the structural chain.

### Why use a mutual fund

- **Diversification** — small ticket buys exposure to many securities.
- **Professional management** — fund managers + research teams.
- **Liquidity** — open-ended schemes redeem within T+2.
- **Regulatory protection** — SEBI-regulated, segregated assets.
- **Tax efficiency** — equity LTCG concession; debt tax structure.
- **Convenience** — SIP, STP, SWP automation.

### Open-ended vs Close-ended

| | Open-ended | Close-ended |
|---|---|---|
| Subscription | Continuous | Only at NFO |
| Redemption | Anytime at NAV | Only at maturity (or via exchange) |
| Listing | Not listed | Listed on exchange |
| Tenor | Perpetual | Fixed (3, 5 yrs typical) |

### SEBI scheme categorisation (Oct 2017 framework)

**Equity (10):** Large Cap, Large & Mid Cap, Mid Cap, Small Cap, Multi Cap, Flexi Cap, Dividend Yield, Value/Contra, Focused, Sectoral/Thematic, ELSS.

**Debt (16):** Overnight, Liquid, Ultra Short, Low Duration, Money Market, Short Duration, Medium, Medium-to-Long, Long, Dynamic Bond, Corporate Bond, Credit Risk, Banking & PSU, Gilt, Gilt 10-yr, Floater.

**Hybrid (7):** Conservative, Balanced, Aggressive, Dynamic Asset Allocation, Multi Asset, Arbitrage, Equity Savings.

**Solution-oriented (2):** Retirement, Children's.

**Other (2):** Index/ETF, Fund of Funds.

### NAV (Net Asset Value)

```
NAV = (Market value of investments + Receivables − Liabilities) / Units outstanding
```

Calculated daily for open-ended; at intervals for close-ended.

### Total Expense Ratio (TER)

SEBI caps TER based on AUM slabs. Higher AUM = lower TER. Open-ended equity (other than FoF/Index/ETF):

| AUM (₹ Cr) | Equity | Other |
|---|---|---|
| First 500 | 2.25% | 2.00% |
| Next 250 | 2.00% | 1.75% |
| Next 1,250 | 1.75% | 1.50% |
| Next 3,000 | 1.60% | 1.35% |
| Above 5,000 | 1.05% | 0.80% |

### SIP, STP, SWP

- **SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)** — invest fixed amount at regular intervals (monthly typical).
- **STP (Systematic Transfer Plan)** — transfer between two MF schemes systematically.
- **SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan)** — withdraw fixed amount regularly (good for retirees).

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **MF benefits = "DPL + RTC"** — Diversification, Professional management, Liquidity + Regulatory protection, Tax efficiency, Convenience.
- **Sponsor → Trust → AMC** structure (next chapter).
- **Open-ended = continuous; Close-ended = fixed tenor + exchange-listed.**
- **NAV = Net assets / Units.** Daily for open-ended.
- **TER falls as AUM grows** — economies of scale baked in.
- **SIP / STP / SWP** = the three systematic plans.
