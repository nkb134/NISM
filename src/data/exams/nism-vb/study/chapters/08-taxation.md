## 🎯 Summary Card

Two big tax buckets for MF gains: **Equity-oriented (≥65% equity)** vs **Non-equity (debt + others)**. **Equity STCG: 20%** (held ≤12 months); **Equity LTCG: 12.5% above ₹1.25 lakh per FY** (held >12 months). **Debt funds bought after April 1, 2023: taxed at slab rate** regardless of holding period (no indexation, no LTCG benefit). **Dividends taxable at slab** in receiver's hands (DDT abolished FY21). **STT** applies on equity MF redemptions.

## 📖 Core Content

### Equity-oriented vs Non-equity

A scheme is **equity-oriented** if it invests **≥65% in domestic equities** (some sub-categories like arbitrage are also classified as equity-oriented). Otherwise it's non-equity (debt, gold, international).

### Capital gains — equity-oriented schemes (post-July 2024)

| Holding period | Tax |
|---|---|
| **≤ 12 months (STCG)** | **20%** flat (post-July 2024) |
| **> 12 months (LTCG)** | **12.5%** on gains above ₹1.25 lakh per FY |

> Pre-July 2024 rates were STCG 15%, LTCG 10%. Updated questions test the new rates.

### Capital gains — debt funds

**Important post-April 2023 change:**
- **Debt funds bought on or after April 1, 2023 → taxed at slab rate**, regardless of holding period.
- **No indexation benefit, no concessional LTCG rate** for these.
- Old debt fund holdings (bought before April 1, 2023) follow the older rules: STCG slab if held ≤36 months; LTCG 20% with indexation if held >36 months.

### Capital gains — gold + international funds

- Treated like debt funds: **slab rate** (post April 2023 acquisitions).
- No LTCG concession.

### Dividends from MFs

- **DDT abolished FY 21.**
- Dividends now taxed at investor's slab rate.
- **TDS at 10% if dividend > ₹5,000 per FY** per investor.

### Securities Transaction Tax (STT)

| Transaction | STT |
|---|---|
| Equity MF redemption | **0.001% on sell value** |
| Debt MF redemption | None (no STT on debt) |

### SIP taxation

Each SIP installment is treated as a separate purchase with its OWN holding period. So for a 5-year SIP, the most-recent installments may still be STCG while older ones are LTCG.

### Switch + STP taxation

A switch (or STP installment) between two schemes is treated as a **redemption + purchase** for tax. STCG/LTCG applies to the redemption side. Common surprise for investors.

### SWP taxation

Each SWP installment is a partial redemption. Capital gains computed proportionally on each withdrawal.

### Dividend reinvestment

Treated as a fresh purchase at the reinvested-NAV. New holding period starts from the reinvestment date.

### Set-off + carry-forward (capital losses)

| Loss type | Set off in same year against | C/F years | C/F set off only against |
|---|---|---|---|
| **STCG loss** | Any capital gain (ST or LT) | 8 | Any capital gain |
| **LTCG loss** | LTCG only | 8 | LTCG only |

### Tax on systematic transfer (STP)

Each STP installment from one scheme to another = redemption from source + purchase in target. Triggers capital-gains tax on source scheme.

### Section 80C — ELSS

- **Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS)** is the only MF category eligible for **Section 80C** deduction (up to ₹1.5 lakh per FY).
- **3-year mandatory lock-in** — shortest among 80C instruments.
- After lock-in, treated as a normal equity scheme for capital gains.

### TCS on MF transactions

For high-value (>₹50 lakh) transactions, TCS provisions may apply per Income-tax Act. Distributors should refer investors to a CA for high-value advice.

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **Equity STCG = 20% (post-July 2024); Equity LTCG = 12.5% above ₹1.25 lakh** per FY (held > 12 months).
- **Debt bought after April 1, 2023 = slab rate, no indexation, no LTCG benefit.**
- **DDT abolished FY 21**; dividends taxed at slab.
- **TDS on dividend > ₹5,000 per FY = 10%.**
- **STT on equity MF redemption = 0.001%; no STT on debt MF.**
- **Switch / STP = redemption + purchase** for tax. Triggers gains.
- **ELSS = only Section 80C MF**; 3-year lock-in.
- **STCG loss can offset any cap gain; LTCG loss only LTCG. Both carry 8 years.**
