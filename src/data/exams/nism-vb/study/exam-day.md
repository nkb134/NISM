# V-B Exam Day Checklist

V-B is the lightest NISM exam — 50 Qs in 75 min, 50% pass mark, **no negative marking**. Most candidates pass on first attempt with one solid week of prep.

## Night before

- **Re-read [Number Sheet](./number-sheet) + [Memory Hooks](./memory-hooks).** No new material.
- Skip a fresh practice mock.
- Light dinner; 7-8 hours sleep.
- Pack:
  - Photo ID (Aadhaar / PAN / DL — same as registration)
  - Admit card (printout or PDF on phone)
  - Pen (centre may not allow your own — verify)
  - **No** smart watch / mobile / notes — locker provided

## Morning of

- **Arrive 30 min early.** ID + biometric verification takes 10 min.
- Glance at [Number Sheet](./number-sheet) in the lobby.
- 2 min calm breathing before screen unlock.

## During the 75 minutes

### Time strategy

- **75 min for 50 Qs = 90 sec / Q on average.**
- Plan: **30 min for first pass** (~36 sec / Q) — answer obvious ones, flag the rest.
- **30 min for second pass** on flagged questions.
- **15 min reserved** for review + the 5-10 toughest you skipped.

### **Critical: NO negative marking → answer every question**

| Confidence | Decision |
|---|---|
| You know it | Answer (+1) |
| Narrowed to 2 options | Answer. EV = +0.5 |
| Narrowed to 3 options | Answer. EV ≈ +0.33 |
| 4-option blind guess | Answer anyway. EV = +0.25 |

**No blanks. Mark something on every question.**

### Reading discipline

- **Read every option** even when (a) looks right.
- **Watch for "NOT", "EXCEPT", "INCORRECT"** in the stem.
- **Flag-and-skip aggressively** — don't burn 60 seconds on one tough question when the next one is easy.

### Topic-by-topic order

If you can navigate freely (you can on Schoolnet):

1. **Pass 1: Chs 1, 4, 6, 8, 10** — vocabulary + memory + regulations, fast.
2. **Pass 2: Chs 2, 3, 5, 7** — concepts + structure + scheme info + performance.
3. **Pass 3: Ch 9** — investor services, the heaviest single chapter.

### Common pitfalls in the moment

- **Tax updates** — make sure you're using post-July 2024 equity rates and post-April 2023 debt rules.
- **NAV cut-off for liquid** — 1:30 PM subscription, 3:00 PM redemption (different from non-liquid).
- **Subscription ≥ ₹2 lakh** — NAV is based on funds-realised date.
- **TER cap reading** — first 500 cr is 2.25% for equity; reads decreasing AUM-wise.
- **₹500** AMC-to-distributor gift cap.
- **0.02%** TER allocation for investor awareness (decimal trap).

## After

- **Don't compare answers** with others.
- **Result is instant** — Schoolnet shows pass/fail at submit.
- Score sheet emailed within minutes.

## If you fail

- 24-hour cool-off.
- Re-attempt window opens immediately on NISM portal.
- Identify weak topics; redo those chapters + topic drills.

## If you pass

- Certificate available on NISM portal within 30 days.
- **Validity: 3 years.** CPE programme to renew.
- V-B is recognised by SEBI for **New-Cadre Distributor** registration (ARN). Update LinkedIn + AMFI portal.

---

**Goal: walk out with at least 35/50** — comfortable above the 25/50 pass mark.
