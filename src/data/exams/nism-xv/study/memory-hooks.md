# XV Memory Hooks

Every mnemonic across the 15 chapters.

## Ch 1 — Research Analyst Profession
- **Three RA types = "SBI"** — Sell-side, Buy-side, Independent.
- **Sell-side public; buy-side internal; independent paid subscription.**
- **Independence + Neutrality** = the most-tested quality.
- **SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014** is the governing rulebook.

## Ch 2 — Securities Market
- **Primary = funds to issuer; Secondary = funds between investors.**
- **NSDL (1996), CDSL (1999)** — two depositories.
- **SEBI / RBI / IRDAI / PFRDA** — capital / banking / insurance / pension.
- **ICDR governs the issue; LODR governs the listed life.**
- **Book-building > Fixed-price** for most IPOs.

## Ch 3 — Terminology
- **EPS / P/E / Dividend yield** — memorise formulas cold.
- **Bond rates inverse rule** — "rates UP, prices DOWN."
- **Modified ≈ Macaulay / (1+YTM).**
- **Convexity is asymmetric** — bond gains more on a yield drop than it loses on an equal yield rise.
- **G-Sec yield curve = the anchor.**
- **AAA = top credit; D = default.**

## Ch 4 — Fundamentals of Research
- **FA looks at the business; TA looks at the chart.**
- **Top-down = E-I-C; Bottom-up = C-I-E.**
- **EMH: Weak → Technical fails; Semi-strong → Fundamental fails; Strong → even insiders fail.**
- **Behavioural biases = "AHRO-LDA"** — Anchoring, Herding, Recency, Overconfidence, Loss-aversion, Disposition, Availability.
- **Primary > Secondary > Tertiary** sources.

## Ch 5 — Economic Analysis
- **GDP, CPI, Repo** — the three you'll see in 80% of macro questions.
- **Fiscal = Govt; Monetary = RBI.**
- **Business cycle = "EPCT"** — Expansion, Peak, Contraction, Trough.
- **Leading vs Lagging** — leading goes BEFORE GDP; lagging comes AFTER.
- **High inflation crushes equity multiples.**
- **FDI = sticky, long-term; FPI = hot money.**
- **Repo cut → 6-12 month lag → real-economy impact.**

## Ch 6 — Industry Analysis
- **Five Forces "RNSSB"** — Rivalry, New entrants, Substitutes, Suppliers, Buyers.
- **PESTLE = "PESTEL"** — same six factors.
- **Life cycle "IGMD"** — Introduction, Growth, Maturity, Decline.
- **Cyclical / Defensive / Growth / Value** — four buckets.
- **HIGH five forces = LOW industry attractiveness.**

## Ch 7 — Business & Governance
- **Moat types = "NBSCRPD"** — Network, Brand, Scale, Cost, Regulatory, Patents, Distribution.
- **Customer concentration > 40% = single-customer risk.**
- **Independent directors:** 1/3 if exec chair, 1/2 if non-exec chair.
- **Audit committee:** ≥ 3 members, 2/3 independent, independent chair.
- **Material RPT > 10% of consolidated turnover** — needs prior shareholder approval.
- **BRSR mandatory for top 1000 listed entities** since FY23.
- **Promoter pledge rising = warning sign.**
- **CFO + CEO exit together = top-tier red flag.**

## Ch 8 — Financial Analysis
- **Three statements: P&L (period), Balance (snapshot), Cash Flow (period).**
- **EBITDA = Earnings Before Interest, Tax, D&A.**
- **FCF = CFO − Capex.**
- **DuPont: NPM × ATO × EM = ROE.** Three drivers explain ROE.
- **Working capital cycle = DSO + Days Inventory − DPO.** Lower is better.
- **CFO consistently < Net Profit = warning sign.**
- **Interest coverage > 4-5× = comfortable; < 2× = stressed.**
- **Common-size statements** are the cheapest spot-check tool.

## Ch 9 — Corporate Actions
- **Bonus + Split don't change market cap** — only the share count.
- **Record date = who's eligible; Ex-date = price drops.**
- **DDT abolished in FY21** — dividends now taxable at slab rate; TDS over ₹5,000.
- **Buyback boosts EPS** by reducing share count.
- **Buyback tax: 23.296% paid by company; recipient tax-free.**
- **Open offer triggered at 25%** stake crossing under SAST Regs.
- **Adjustment factor = Price before / Price after.**
- **Dividend > 5% → adjust strike;** otherwise ignore for derivatives.

## Ch 10 — Valuation
- **Absolute vs Relative.** Always do at least one of each.
- **WACC = blend of equity + after-tax debt cost.**
- **CAPM: Rf + β × ERP.** Three inputs, all observable.
- **Gordon: V = D₁ / (r − g).** Constraint: g < r.
- **DCF terminal value > 70% = warning sign.** Be conservative on g.
- **EV/EBITDA for capex-heavy; P/E for mature; P/B for financials; P/Sales for early-stage.**
- **Triangulate.** Single-method valuation is brittle.
- **Margin of safety = buy 30-50% below intrinsic.**

## Ch 11 — Commodities
- **Commodities have no earnings.** Demand-supply only.
- **Three families: Agri, Metals, Energy.**
- **Stronger USD = lower commodity prices.**
- **Contango = futures > spot; Backwardation = spot > futures.**
- **MCX = metals + energy; NCDEX = agri.**
- **"Dr. Copper" leads global growth.**
- **Crude ↑ → INR ↓** (India imports ~85% of crude).
- **Negative roll yield** in contango can kill long futures.

## Ch 12 — Risk & Return
- **Geometric mean ≤ Arithmetic mean** always. Geometric = what you actually earn.
- **σ measures total risk; β measures systematic risk only.**
- **Diversification kills unsystematic risk; can't touch systematic.**
- **Sharpe = total-risk denominator; Treynor = systematic-risk denominator.**
- **Jensen's alpha > 0 = manager beat CAPM expectation.**
- **CAPM: Rf + β × ERP.**
- **Lower ρ between assets = more diversification benefit.**
- **Markowitz Efficient Frontier + Capital Market Line = MPT core.**

## Ch 13 — Research Report Quality
- **Lead with the call.** First page = recommendation + thesis + target.
- **3-5 bullets is the right thesis length.**
- **Disclose conflicts upfront.** SEBI mandates 7+ specific disclosures.
- **Reports retained 5 years.**
- **Cooling-off ≈ 30 days** before analyst can trade the recommended stock.
- **Distribution must be simultaneous** to all entitled clients.
- **Quantified risks > vague risks.**
- **Initiation = 30-50 pages; quarterly note = 4-8 pages; daily = 1-2.**

## Ch 14 — Legal & Regulatory
- **SEBI (RA) Regulations 2014** = the governing rulebook.
- **NISM-XV mandatory** for the RA + every research employee.
- **Net worth: ₹1 lakh individual / ₹25 lakh entity.**
- **5 years record retention.**
- **Cooling-off ~30 days** for RA's own trades.
- **PIT 2015 = insider trading; PFUTP 2003 = market manipulation.**
- **Penalty cap = ₹25 cr or 3× profits** (whichever higher).
- **Trading window closes pre-results** (T-7 to T+2).
- **Annual SEBI inspection.**
- **Grievance: RA compliance → SCORES → SMARTODR.**

## Ch 15 — Technical Analysis
- **Dow Theory: discount, trend, history.**
- **Candlestick = body + wicks.** Bull green; Bear red.
- **Hammer at support = bullish; Shooting Star at resistance = bearish.**
- **Engulfing = strong reversal.**
- **Support flips to resistance after a break.**
- **RSI > 70 = overbought; RSI < 30 = oversold.**
- **MACD crossover above signal = bullish.**
- **Bollinger squeeze = imminent big move.**
- **H&S = bearish reversal; Inverse H&S = bullish reversal.**
- **Fibonacci: 23.6, 38.2, 50, 61.8, 78.6.**
- **Elliott: 5 impulse + 3 corrective waves.**
- **TA = WHEN; FA = WHAT.**
