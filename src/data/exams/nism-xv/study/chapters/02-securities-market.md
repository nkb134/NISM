## 🎯 Summary Card

Securities market = **primary** (issuer raises new capital) + **secondary** (existing securities trade). Three asset classes: **equity, debt, hybrid** (convertibles, preference). Regulators: **SEBI** (capital markets), **RBI** (G-secs, currency, money market), **IRDAI** (insurance), **PFRDA** (pensions). Key intermediaries: **stock exchanges, depositories (NSDL/CDSL), clearing corps, brokers, RTAs, custodians, merchant bankers, AMCs**. Listing requires **SEBI ICDR Regulations** compliance.

## 📖 Core Content

### Primary vs secondary

| | Primary | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| What | New issue of securities | Trade in existing securities |
| Funds flow | To issuer | Between investors |
| Examples | IPO, FPO, rights, private placement | Cash market, F&O, debt OTC |

### Securities types (SCRA Section 2(h))

- **Shares** — equity, preference (cumulative / non-cumulative, redeemable / convertible).
- **Debt** — bonds, debentures, NCDs, T-bills, G-secs, commercial paper.
- **Derivatives** — futures, options, swaps.
- **Mutual fund units, REITs, InvITs.**
- **Government securities, security receipts, onshore rupee bonds** by IFC/ADB.

### Indian regulators (don't confuse)

| Regulator | Domain |
|---|---|
| **SEBI** | Capital markets, intermediaries, mutual funds, FPIs |
| **RBI** | Banking, G-secs, currency, money market, payment systems |
| **IRDAI** | Insurance industry |
| **PFRDA** | Pension funds (NPS, APY) |
| **MCA** | Companies Act compliance |

### Key intermediaries

- **Stock Exchanges** — NSE, BSE, MSEI; trading venue.
- **Depositories** — **NSDL** (1996) and **CDSL** (1999); hold demat securities.
- **Clearing Corporations** — NCL (NSE), ICCL (BSE), MCCIL (MSEI); novate trades.
- **Stock Brokers + Authorised Persons** — execute trades for clients.
- **RTAs (Registrar & Transfer Agents)** — maintain investor records.
- **Custodians** — safekeep institutional holdings.
- **Merchant Bankers** — manage IPOs, FPOs, M&A.
- **AMCs (Asset Management Companies)** — manage mutual fund schemes.
- **Investment Advisers + Research Analysts** — separate SEBI registration regimes.

### Primary-market routes

- **IPO (Initial Public Offer)** — first-time public issue.
- **FPO (Follow-on Public Offer)** — already-listed company issues more.
- **Rights issue** — to existing shareholders pro-rata.
- **Bonus issue** — free shares from reserves; no cash.
- **Private placement / preferential allotment / QIP** — to selected institutional investors.

### Book-building vs fixed-price

- **Book-building** — price band, investors bid, final price = cut-off determined from demand. Most IPOs use this.
- **Fixed-price** — issuer sets price upfront. Smaller / SME issues sometimes use.

### Listing rules

Governed by **SEBI ICDR Regulations** (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) + **LODR Regulations** (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements). Listed companies must publish **quarterly results, annual report, material disclosures** within prescribed timelines.

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **Primary = funds to issuer; Secondary = funds between investors.**
- **NSDL (1996), CDSL (1999)** — two depositories.
- **SEBI / RBI / IRDAI / PFRDA** — capital / banking / insurance / pension.
- **ICDR governs the issue; LODR governs the listed life.**
- **Book-building > Fixed-price** for most IPOs — price discovered from bids.
