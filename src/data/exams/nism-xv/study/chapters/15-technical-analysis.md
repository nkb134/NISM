## 🎯 Summary Card

Technical analysis (TA) reads **price + volume** to predict direction. Three Dow Theory tenets: **markets discount everything, prices move in trends, history repeats**. Charts: **line, bar, candlestick** (most popular). Key concepts: **support, resistance, trend lines, channels**. Indicators split into **trend-following** (MA, MACD), **momentum** (RSI, Stochastics), **volatility** (Bollinger Bands, ATR), **volume** (OBV). Patterns: **head-and-shoulders, double top/bottom, triangles, flags**. **Elliott Wave** + **Fibonacci retracements** = advanced. TA works because of behavioural-pattern persistence, not magic.

## 📖 Core Content

### Dow Theory — TA's foundation

1. **Markets discount everything** — prices already reflect all known information.
2. **Markets move in three trends** — primary (months/years), secondary (weeks), minor (days).
3. **Trends have three phases** — accumulation (smart money buys), public participation (trend obvious), distribution (smart money sells).
4. **Indices must confirm each other** — e.g. Sensex + Nifty + sector indices align.
5. **Volume confirms the trend** — rising prices on rising volume = healthy.
6. **A trend continues until reversal is signalled** — don't anticipate reversals; wait for confirmation.

### Chart types

| Chart | Shows | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Line chart** | Closing prices only | Long-term overview |
| **Bar (OHLC)** | Open / High / Low / Close as a vertical bar | Period detail |
| **Candlestick** | Same as OHLC but coloured body shows up/down day | **Most popular**; pattern reading |
| **Renko / P&F** | Filter out time; only price moves matter | Trend-only views |

### Candlestick anatomy

```
Upper wick (high)
─┬─
 │     ← Body (open to close)
 │
─┴─
Lower wick (low)

Bullish (green/white): Close > Open
Bearish (red/black):  Close < Open
```

### Single-candle patterns (high-yield)

| Pattern | Signal |
|---|---|
| **Doji** | Open ≈ Close; indecision; reversal possible after a strong trend |
| **Hammer** | Small body at top + long lower wick; bullish reversal at support |
| **Shooting Star** | Small body at bottom + long upper wick; bearish reversal at resistance |
| **Marubozu** | Body fills the entire range; strong directional momentum |
| **Spinning Top** | Small body, both wicks; indecision |

### Two-candle patterns

| Pattern | Signal |
|---|---|
| **Bullish Engulfing** | Small bear candle then large bull candle that engulfs it; strong reversal up |
| **Bearish Engulfing** | Mirror; strong reversal down |
| **Piercing Pattern** | Bear candle, then bull candle closing above midpoint of bear; bullish |
| **Dark Cloud Cover** | Mirror; bearish |

### Three-candle patterns

| Pattern | Signal |
|---|---|
| **Morning Star** | Bear → small body (gap) → bull; bullish reversal |
| **Evening Star** | Bull → small body (gap) → bear; bearish reversal |
| **Three White Soldiers** | Three consecutive bull candles; strong uptrend |
| **Three Black Crows** | Mirror; strong downtrend |

### Support + resistance

- **Support** — price level where buying interest historically halted declines.
- **Resistance** — level where selling halted advances.
- After a break, **support flips to resistance** (and vice versa).
- Drawn from prior price highs / lows, round numbers, moving averages.

### Trend lines + channels

- **Uptrend line** — connect at least 2 (better 3) higher lows.
- **Downtrend line** — connect lower highs.
- **Channel** — parallel line on the opposite side; price oscillates within.
- Trend-line break with volume = directional signal.

### Trend-following indicators

| Indicator | Calculation | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple Moving Average (SMA)** | Avg of last N closes | Price > SMA = uptrend; below = downtrend |
| **Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** | Weighted SMA, recent prices weighted more | Faster than SMA |
| **MACD** | EMA(12) − EMA(26); signal = EMA(9) of MACD | MACD crossing above signal = bullish |
| **ADX (Average Directional Index)** | Trend strength (not direction) | ADX > 25 = strong trend; < 20 = no trend |

### Momentum indicators (oscillators)

| Indicator | Range | Overbought / Oversold |
|---|---|---|
| **RSI (Relative Strength Index)** | 0-100 | > 70 OB; < 30 OS |
| **Stochastic Oscillator (%K, %D)** | 0-100 | > 80 OB; < 20 OS |
| **CCI (Commodity Channel Index)** | Unbounded | > +100 OB; < −100 OS |
| **Williams %R** | -100 to 0 | > -20 OB; < -80 OS |

### Volatility indicators

- **Bollinger Bands** — 20-period SMA ± 2 standard deviations. Price near upper band = stretched; lower = oversold. **Squeeze** (bands narrowing) precedes a big move.
- **ATR (Average True Range)** — average daily price range over N days. Measures volatility magnitude, not direction.

### Volume indicators

- **OBV (On-Balance Volume)** — cumulative volume; up days add, down days subtract. Rising OBV with rising price = healthy uptrend.
- **A/D Line (Accumulation/Distribution)** — refined OBV using close vs day's range.

### Chart patterns (high-yield)

| Pattern | Continuation or Reversal | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Head and Shoulders** | Reversal | After uptrend, three peaks (middle highest); break below "neckline" = bearish |
| **Inverse H&S** | Reversal | Mirror of above; bullish reversal |
| **Double Top / Double Bottom** | Reversal | Two peaks (or troughs) at similar level; reversal on neckline break |
| **Triangle (Asc / Desc / Sym)** | Continuation usually | Converging trend lines; breakout direction continues prior trend |
| **Flag / Pennant** | Continuation | Sharp move + brief consolidation + continuation |
| **Cup and Handle** | Continuation (bullish) | U-shape + small dip; breakout above handle |
| **Wedge (Rising / Falling)** | Reversal | Both lines slope same direction but converging |

### Fibonacci retracements

After a price move, retracement levels at **23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, 78.6%** of the prior move are common bounce / pullback zones.

### Elliott Wave (advanced)

- Markets move in **5 impulse waves** (with the trend) + **3 corrective waves** (against the trend) — 5-3 structure.
- Wave 3 is typically the longest + strongest.
- Fractal structure — same pattern repeats at all timeframes.

### Combining TA with FA

- **FA tells you WHAT** to buy/sell (intrinsic value).
- **TA tells you WHEN** to act (timing).
- Best practice: shortlist by FA, time entries / exits with TA.
- Single approach is fragile; combination beats either alone.

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **Dow Theory: discount, trend, history.** Three pillars.
- **Candlestick = body + wicks.** Bull = green; Bear = red.
- **Hammer at support = bullish; Shooting Star at resistance = bearish.**
- **Engulfing patterns = strong reversal.**
- **Support flips to resistance after a break** (and vice versa).
- **RSI > 70 = overbought; RSI < 30 = oversold.** Same idea for Stochastic at 80/20.
- **MACD crossover above signal = bullish.**
- **Bollinger squeeze = imminent big move.**
- **H&S = bearish reversal; Inverse H&S = bullish reversal.**
- **Fibonacci retracements: 23.6, 38.2, 50, 61.8, 78.6.**
- **Elliott: 5 impulse + 3 corrective waves.**
- **TA = WHEN; FA = WHAT.** Use both.
