## 🎯 Summary Card

Industry sits between economy and company. Two frameworks: **Porter's Five Forces** (competitive intensity) + **PESTLE** (Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental). **Industry life cycle**: Introduction → Growth → Maturity → Decline. Classify industries as **cyclical, defensive, growth, or value**. Drivers vary: capex cycle, regulation, technology disruption, consumer behaviour, raw-material costs. Compare via **market share, growth rate, margin profile, ROCE, capex intensity**.

## 📖 Core Content

### Porter's Five Forces

| Force | Question |
|---|---|
| **1. Industry rivalry** | How intense is competition among existing players? |
| **2. Threat of new entrants** | How easy is it for new players to enter? |
| **3. Threat of substitutes** | Can buyers switch to alternative products / technologies? |
| **4. Bargaining power of suppliers** | Can suppliers raise prices / restrict supply? |
| **5. Bargaining power of buyers** | Can buyers force prices down? |

High forces ↓ industry attractiveness. Low forces ↑ pricing power → higher margins → ROCE.

### PESTLE framework

| Factor | Examples |
|---|---|
| **P**olitical | Govt stability, foreign policy, election cycles |
| **E**conomic | GDP, inflation, interest rates, FX |
| **S**ocial | Demographics, urbanisation, lifestyle shifts |
| **T**echnological | R&D pace, automation, digitisation |
| **L**egal | Compliance, IP protection, labour laws |
| **E**nvironmental | Climate risk, ESG mandates, resource scarcity |

### Industry life cycle

| Stage | Characteristics | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **Introduction** | Few firms, R&D heavy, losses, low awareness | Brand-building, IP capture |
| **Growth** | Rising demand, new entrants, capex surge | Capacity scaling |
| **Maturity** | Slow growth, consolidation, margin compression | Efficiency, M&A |
| **Decline** | Falling demand, exits | Cost cuts, divestiture |

### Industry classifications

- **Cyclical** — sensitive to macro: autos, steel, cement, capital goods. Outperform in expansion.
- **Defensive** — stable demand: FMCG, pharma, utilities. Outperform in contraction.
- **Growth** — high earnings expansion: tech, fintech, healthcare. Premium multiples.
- **Value** — mature, dividend-paying, low growth: PSUs, traditional manufacturing.

### Key metrics for industry comparison

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Industry **growth rate** | Top-line momentum |
| **Market share** distribution | Competitive concentration; HHI index |
| **Aggregate margin profile** (gross / operating / net) | Pricing power |
| **ROCE / ROE** at the industry level | Capital efficiency |
| **Capex intensity** (Capex / Sales) | Reinvestment requirement |
| **Working capital cycle** | Cash conversion efficiency |

### Industry-specific drivers (examples)

| Industry | Top drivers |
|---|---|
| **Auto** | Interest rates, fuel prices, urban income, regulation (BS norms, EVs) |
| **IT services** | USD/INR, US/EU IT spend, immigration policy, AI disruption |
| **Banks** | NIM, credit growth, NPA cycle, RBI rate policy |
| **Pharma** | USFDA inspections, generic price erosion, R&D pipeline |
| **FMCG** | Rural / urban consumption, raw-material costs (palm oil, packaging) |
| **Cement** | Infrastructure spend, real estate cycle, fuel costs |

### SWOT layered on industry

For a quick industry snapshot:
- **Strengths** of the industry collectively
- **Weaknesses** (over-capacity, regulatory risk)
- **Opportunities** (demographic dividend, govt stimulus)
- **Threats** (substitutes, foreign competition)

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **Five Forces "RNSSB"** — Rivalry, New entrants, Substitutes, Suppliers, Buyers.
- **PESTLE = "PESTEL"** depending on the textbook — same six factors.
- **Life cycle "IGMD"** — Introduction, Growth, Maturity, Decline.
- **Cyclical vs Defensive vs Growth vs Value** — the four buckets every analyst uses.
- **HIGH five forces = LOW industry attractiveness.**
- **Industry analysis sits between macro (Ch 5) and company (Chs 7-8).**
