## 🎯 Summary Card

A **Research Analyst (RA)** collects + analyses data on economies, industries, companies and recommends **buy / hold / sell**. SEBI defines RA in **SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014**. Three coverage models: **sell-side** (public reports for clients), **buy-side** (internal-only for asset managers), **independent** (subscription / boutique). Key qualities: number-fluency, financial-statement comprehension, modelling skill, clear writing, **independence + neutrality**, ethical conduct.

## 📖 Core Content

### What an RA does

- **Research:** gather macro / micro / industry / company data from RBI, IMF, ADB, World Bank, industry journals, annual reports, channel checks.
- **Analysis:** combine **quantitative** (revenues, costs, margins, cash flows) + **qualitative** (management quality, brand, competitive moats, work ethics) factors.
- **Output:** a recommendation — buy, hold, or sell — with a **price target** and time horizon.

### The three RA categories

| Type | Audience | Reports |
|---|---|---|
| **Sell-side** | Public, brokerage clients | Published, often free |
| **Buy-side** | Internal — fund managers, PMS | Confidential |
| **Independent** | Subscribers, institutions | Paid; sometimes custom |

### Principles for company / client interaction

- **Pre-meeting research** — read annual reports, know products, competitors.
- **Independence + neutrality** — opinion based on facts; refuse non-public information.
- **Networking** — speak to suppliers, distributors, retailers, customers — not just top management.
- **Clear questions** — go with a structured questionnaire.
- **Disclosure** — declare conflicts of interest (e.g. RA holds shares).
- **Plain language** — no unexplained jargon; spell out assumptions.
- **Honesty + ethics** — no painted-rosy pictures; cross-verify management claims.

### Qualities of a good RA

- Numeric fluency, Excel / data-analysis tooling.
- Read + comprehend financial statements deeply.
- Understand business models + competitive dynamics.
- Methodical, enquiring, discerning about source quality.
- Excellent written communication — research reports must be simple, clear, concise.
- Skeptical, independent, ethically grounded.

### Communication with clients

- **Realistic** suggestions based on facts.
- **Conflict-of-interest disclosure** upfront.
- **Assumptions stated clearly.**
- Avoid abbreviations; if used, explain.
- Recording with consent is fine for accuracy.

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **RA = Researcher + Analyst.** Two halves: gather data, then judge.
- **Three RA types = "SBI"** — Sell-side, Buy-side, Independent.
- **Sell-side reports go public; buy-side stays internal; independent is paid subscription.**
- **Independence + Neutrality** is the most-tested quality. Reject non-public info; disclose conflicts.
- **SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014** is the parent rulebook.
