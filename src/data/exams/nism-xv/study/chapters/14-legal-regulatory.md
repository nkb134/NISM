## 🎯 Summary Card

The **SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014** are the governing rulebook. Mandatory **SEBI registration**; minimum **NISM-XV certification**; net-worth requirement (₹1 lakh individual / ₹25 lakh body corporate); **separation from broking + investment banking**. Strict rules on **independence, conflict-of-interest disclosure, personal trading** (cooling-off), **research report integrity**. Insider trading, fraud, market manipulation are **separately prohibited under SEBI Acts + PIT Regulations 2015**. Distribution + record-keeping (5 years) are mandatory.

## 📖 Core Content

### SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014 — the spine

**Who needs to register:**
- Anyone who, **for consideration**, prepares or publishes research reports / recommendations on listed securities.
- Includes both individuals and entities.
- **Exemption:** journalists writing in newspapers/media (provided no personal compensation tied to recommendations).

**Eligibility for registration:**
| Requirement | Threshold |
|---|---|
| **NISM-XV certification** | Mandatory for the RA + every research employee |
| **Educational qualification** | Min graduation; postgraduation in finance/economics/CA/CFA/CS/MBA preferred |
| **Net worth — individual** | ≥ ₹1 lakh |
| **Net worth — body corporate** | ≥ ₹25 lakh |
| **Experience** | 5 years for principal officer (entity) |
| **Fit & proper** | No prior conviction/regulatory action |

### Code of Conduct (Schedule III)

- **Honesty + integrity** in all dealings.
- **Independence + impartiality** — no opinion shaping by issuer / employer pressures.
- **Due skill, care, diligence** in preparing reports.
- **Disclosure of conflicts** — financial interest, family holdings, firm's IB relationship.
- **Confidentiality** of non-public client information.
- **Compliance** with applicable laws + SEBI directives.
- **Fair representation** — no material omissions, no misleading claims.

### Mandatory disclosures (in every report)

1. RA's **SEBI registration number**.
2. **Personal holdings** of the subject security (analyst + immediate family).
3. **Firm's holdings** if > 1% of subject company's equity.
4. **Material conflict of interest** of any kind.
5. **Compensation received** from the subject company in past 12 months.
6. **Past recommendations** + outcomes (link).
7. **Investment banking / advisory relationship** with subject in past 12 months.
8. Standard **disclaimer** + risk warning.

### Personal trading rules

- **Cooling-off period:** RA cannot trade in a recommended security for **30 days before + 5 days after** issuing a recommendation (specific timelines per SEBI; check current circular).
- **No front-running** — trading ahead of the report's release.
- **No tipping** — sharing the call before publication.
- **Pre-clearance** of personal trades by compliance.
- **Complete personal-trading register** maintained for inspection.

### Separation from other activities

A registered RA must:
- **Disclose** if also acting as broker, IB, investment adviser.
- Maintain **arm's-length** between research and any client-facing business.
- **Chinese walls** between research and proprietary trading desks.
- Compensation of analysts must NOT be directly linked to any specific investment-banking transaction.

### Insider trading + UPSI (PIT Regulations 2015)

- **Insider** = anyone in possession of **Unpublished Price Sensitive Information (UPSI)**.
- **UPSI** = info likely to materially affect price (financial results, M&A, dividends, leadership changes, regulatory actions).
- **Trading window** closes pre-results announcement (typically T-7 to T+2 days).
- Penalties: up to **₹25 cr or 3× profit**, whichever higher; criminal prosecution possible.
- Insiders + designated persons must maintain **trading plans** for compliant trading.

### Prohibition of fraud + market manipulation (PFUTP 2003)

The SEBI (Prohibition of Fraudulent and Unfair Trade Practices) Regulations 2003 prohibit:
- **Misleading statements** about security values.
- **Wash trades, circular trades** to inflate volume.
- **Front-running, scalping** — trading ahead of clients.
- **Spreading false rumours**.
- **Spoofing** — placing fake orders to manipulate price.

Penalties up to **₹25 cr or 3× ill-gotten gain**.

### Record keeping

- **5 years** retention of:
  - Research reports + supporting workings.
  - Communications with subject company.
  - Personal trading records.
  - Client distribution lists.
  - Compliance approvals.

### SEBI inspection + enforcement

- **Annual inspection** by SEBI of registered RAs / firms.
- Routine review of report quality, disclosures, personal trading patterns.
- Enforcement actions: **warning → monetary penalty → suspension of registration → cancellation**.
- Public disclosure of enforcement orders on SEBI website.

### Investor grievance handling

- RA must have a **published mechanism** for client complaints.
- 30-day response time.
- Escalation path: complainant → RA's compliance officer → SEBI's **SCORES portal** → SEBI's **SMARTODR** for unresolved disputes.

### Recent updates (typically tested)

- **2025 amendments** tightened net-worth + experience norms; expanded scope to include certain online platforms recommending stocks.
- **AI-generated content disclosure** — if material content of a report is AI-generated, that must be disclosed.
- **BRSR-linked reports** — for ESG-focused research, must reference the company's BRSR filing.
