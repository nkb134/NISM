## 🎯 Summary Card

Commodities = physical goods. Three families: **agri** (wheat, sugar, cotton, soybean), **metals** (gold, silver, copper, aluminium, zinc), **energy** (crude oil, natural gas). **Demand-supply** is the dominant driver — unlike equities, commodities have no "earnings". Key inputs: **production cycles, weather, OPEC decisions, USD, inventory levels, geopolitical events**. Use **forward curve (contango / backwardation)** to read market sentiment. **MCX** + NCDEX are India's main commodity exchanges.

## 📖 Core Content

### Three commodity families

| Family | Examples | Key drivers |
|---|---|---|
| **Agri** | Wheat, sugar, cotton, soybean, palm oil | Monsoon, yield, govt MSP, exports |
| **Metals** | Gold, silver, copper, aluminium, zinc, nickel | Industrial demand, USD, mine supply, China |
| **Energy** | Crude oil, natural gas | OPEC supply, geopolitics, refining margins |

### Why analyse commodities differently from equities

- **No earnings, no dividends** — value comes from supply-demand for the physical good.
- **Cyclical + volatile** — boom-bust cycles tied to capex investment lags.
- **Storage costs matter** — for agri + metals, holding inventory has real cost.
- **Most are USD-denominated** globally — exchange-rate effects compound.

### Demand drivers

| Commodity | Demand source |
|---|---|
| **Crude oil** | Transportation (60%+), petrochemicals, heating |
| **Gold** | Jewellery (India + China = ~50%), central bank reserves, investment, electronics |
| **Copper** | Construction, electrification, EVs (3-4× more vs ICE) |
| **Aluminium** | Auto, packaging, construction, aviation |
| **Iron ore + coal** | Steel-making (Chinese demand cycle) |
| **Wheat / rice** | Food consumption, govt procurement at MSP |

### Supply drivers

- **Mine / well capex** — long lead times (5-10 yrs for new copper mine).
- **OPEC+ quota decisions** — for crude oil, monthly meetings move price.
- **Weather** — monsoon for India agri; hurricanes for US energy infrastructure.
- **Strikes / disruptions** — Chile copper strikes, US oil refinery outages.
- **Govt policy** — export bans, MSP changes.

### The USD inverse correlation

Most global commodities are quoted in USD. **Stronger USD → cheaper commodities for non-USD buyers → demand falls → prices fall.** Inverse holds for weak USD.

### Inventory + the futures curve

```
Contango     = Future price > Spot price       (carry costs > convenience yield)
Backwardation = Future price < Spot price       (tight supply / immediate demand)
```

- **Contango** is the normal state for storable commodities (oil, gold) — reflects storage + financing costs.
- **Backwardation** signals scarcity; usually bullish for the commodity.

### Indian commodity exchanges

| Exchange | Specialisation |
|---|---|
| **MCX** | Energy, metals, bullion (largest) |
| **NCDEX** | Agri commodities |
| **ICEX** | Diamonds, agri (smaller) |

Regulated by **SEBI** (commodity derivatives merged into SEBI's mandate from 2015 — earlier under FMC).

### Cross-commodity analysis

- **Crude oil ↑ → INR weakens** (India is a major crude importer).
- **Gold ↑ → typically when real yields ↓ + USD ↓ + geopolitical risk ↑**.
- **Copper "Dr. Copper"** — leading indicator of global growth (industrial demand).
- **Steel ↑ requires iron ore + coking coal ↑** in lockstep.

### Tools for commodity research

- **Inventory data** — US EIA crude inventories, LME warehouse stocks, US grain stocks.
- **Position data** — CFTC Commitments of Traders (COT) — speculator vs hedger positioning.
- **Government reports** — USDA crop reports, monthly OPEC reports, Indian Met Department forecasts.
- **Industry trade press** — Argus Media, Platts, Kitco for spot quotes.

### Risk factors specific to commodities

- **Margin calls** during sharp moves (commodities can gap 5-10% on news).
- **Curve flattening** — even if you're right on direction, contango can erode rolling-forward returns ("negative roll yield").
- **Storage risk** for physical positions.
- **Black swans** — pandemics (oil 2020), wars (wheat 2022), supply disruptions.

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **Commodities have no earnings.** Demand-supply is the only valuation lens.
- **Three families: Agri, Metals, Energy.**
- **Stronger USD = lower commodity prices** (and vice versa).
- **Contango = futures > spot** (normal); **Backwardation = spot > futures** (tight market).
- **MCX = metals + energy; NCDEX = agri.**
- **"Dr. Copper" leads global growth** — because of industrial use.
- **Crude ↑ → INR ↓** (India imports ~85% of crude).
- **Negative roll yield** in contango can kill long futures positions even if spot is flat.
