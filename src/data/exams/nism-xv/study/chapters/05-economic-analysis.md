## 🎯 Summary Card

Economic analysis sets the macro stage. Key indicators: **GDP, inflation (CPI/WPI), interest rates (repo, reverse repo, MSF), unemployment, fiscal deficit, current account deficit**. Two policy levers: **fiscal** (govt spending + taxation) + **monetary** (RBI rates + liquidity). Business cycle: **expansion → peak → contraction → trough**. Leading / lagging / coincident indicators predict, lag, or move with the cycle.

## 📖 Core Content

### Core macro indicators

| Indicator | What it measures | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **GDP** | Total output; growth = economic momentum | NSO, MoSPI |
| **CPI** | Consumer Price Index; retail inflation | NSO |
| **WPI** | Wholesale Price Index; producer prices | DPIIT |
| **Repo rate** | RBI's key lending rate to banks | RBI |
| **Reverse repo** | Rate RBI pays banks to park funds | RBI |
| **MSF** | Marginal Standing Facility — emergency lending | RBI |
| **CRR + SLR** | Cash + statutory liquidity ratios | RBI |
| **IIP** | Industrial production index | NSO |
| **PMI** | Purchasing Managers' Index — early activity gauge | S&P Global / IHS |
| **Fiscal deficit** | Govt spending − revenue | Union Budget |
| **Current account deficit** | Net imports + transfers | RBI |
| **Forex reserves** | RBI-held FX | RBI |
| **Unemployment rate** | % of labour force unemployed | NSO PLFS, CMIE |

### Fiscal vs Monetary policy

| | Fiscal | Monetary |
|---|---|---|
| Authority | Central / state govt | RBI |
| Tool | Spending, taxation, transfers | Repo, CRR, SLR, OMO |
| Speed | Slow (Budget cycle) | Fast (MPC every 2 months) |
| Direct effect | Demand-side | Liquidity, cost of credit |
| Risk | Higher fiscal deficit | Inflation if too loose |

### Business cycle phases

```
Expansion → Peak → Contraction (Recession) → Trough → Expansion …
```

- **Expansion** — GDP rising, low unemployment, credit easy, equities rally.
- **Peak** — overheating, inflation pressure, rate hikes begin.
- **Contraction** — GDP falls, layoffs, defensive sectors outperform.
- **Trough** — bottom; central bank cuts rates; stage set for recovery.

### Leading / lagging / coincident indicators

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Leading** | Stock market, PMI, building permits, money supply (M3) |
| **Coincident** | GDP, IIP, employment, retail sales |
| **Lagging** | Unemployment rate, CPI, average duration of unemployment |

### Inflation regimes + impact on equities

- **Mild inflation (2-4%)** — supportive for growth + earnings.
- **High inflation (>6%)** — RBI hikes rates → cost of capital up → equity multiples compress.
- **Deflation** — rare in India; signals demand collapse.

### Interest rate transmission

```
RBI Repo cut → Banks lower lending rates → Borrowing rises → 
Investment + consumption rise → GDP grows → Earnings rise → Equities rally
```

Lag is typically 6-12 months from policy action to real impact.

### Foreign capital flows

- **FDI (Foreign Direct Investment)** — long-term, into businesses; sticky.
- **FPI (Foreign Portfolio Investment)** — short-term, into listed securities; volatile.
- FPI flows often correlate with global risk-on/risk-off cycles.

### Currency + commodity dynamics

- **INR weakening** → exports gain; imports + import-dependent sectors hurt.
- **Crude oil ↑** → fuel inflation, current account deficit widens.
- **Gold price** = inverse of real yields (often).

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **GDP, CPI, Repo** — the three you'll see in 80% of macro questions.
- **Fiscal = Govt; Monetary = RBI.** Don't confuse the actor.
- **Business cycle = "EPCT"** — Expansion, Peak, Contraction, Trough.
- **Leading vs Lagging** — leading INDICATORS go BEFORE GDP; lagging indicators come AFTER.
- **High inflation crushes equity multiples.** Always.
- **FDI = sticky, long-term; FPI = hot money.**
- **Repo cut → 6-12 months lag → real-economy impact.**
