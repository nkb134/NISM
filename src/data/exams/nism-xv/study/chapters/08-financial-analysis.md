## 🎯 Summary Card

The quantitative half of company analysis — read **three statements** (P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow) + key ratios. **Five ratio families: profitability, liquidity, leverage, efficiency, valuation**. **DuPont decomposition: ROE = NPM × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier.** Cash flow quality matters more than reported profit. Watch for **non-cash earnings, capitalisation games, off-balance-sheet liabilities, working-capital manipulation**.

## 📖 Core Content

### The three financial statements

| Statement | What it shows | Period |
|---|---|---|
| **P&L (Income Statement)** | Revenue → costs → profit | Period (quarter/year) |
| **Balance Sheet** | Assets, liabilities, equity | Snapshot date |
| **Cash Flow Statement** | Cash in / out across operating, investing, financing | Period |

Linkage: **Net Profit (P&L)** flows into Reserves (BS); **Closing Cash (CF)** = Cash on BS.

### Reading the P&L

Top to bottom:
```
Revenue / Sales
−  COGS (Cost of Goods Sold)
=  Gross Profit              → Gross Margin %
−  Operating Expenses (SG&A, R&D)
=  EBITDA                    → EBITDA Margin %
−  Depreciation + Amortisation
=  EBIT (Operating Profit)   → Operating Margin %
−  Interest Expense
=  PBT (Profit Before Tax)
−  Tax
=  PAT (Net Profit)          → Net Margin %
÷  Diluted shares = Diluted EPS
```

### Reading the Balance Sheet (Indian Schedule III format)

**Equity + Liabilities:**
- Equity: Share capital + Reserves + Surplus
- Non-current liabilities: Long-term debt, deferred tax, provisions
- Current liabilities: Trade payables, short-term debt, accruals

**Assets:**
- Non-current: PP&E (gross block − accumulated depreciation), goodwill, intangibles, investments
- Current: Inventory, trade receivables, cash, short-term investments

### Cash Flow Statement (CFS) — three buckets

| Bucket | What |
|---|---|
| **Operating (CFO)** | Cash from core business — most important |
| **Investing (CFI)** | Capex, acquisitions, divestitures |
| **Financing (CFF)** | Debt raised/repaid, equity issued/buyback, dividends |

**Free Cash Flow (FCF) = CFO − Capex.** FCF is what's available to debt + equity providers.

### Five ratio families

#### 1. Profitability

| Ratio | Formula | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Gross margin | Gross profit / Revenue | Pricing power + cost control |
| Operating margin (EBIT) | EBIT / Revenue | Core ops efficiency |
| EBITDA margin | EBITDA / Revenue | Adds back non-cash D&A |
| Net margin | PAT / Revenue | After interest + tax |
| ROE | PAT / Avg Equity | Return for equity holders |
| ROCE | EBIT / (Equity + Long-term Debt) | Return on total capital employed |
| ROA | Net Income / Avg Total Assets | Return on the asset base |

#### 2. Liquidity

| Ratio | Formula | Healthy range |
|---|---|---|
| Current ratio | Current Assets / Current Liabilities | > 1.5 |
| Quick ratio | (CA − Inventory) / CL | > 1.0 |
| Cash ratio | Cash / CL | > 0.5 |

#### 3. Leverage

| Ratio | Formula |
|---|---|
| Debt / Equity (D/E) | Total Debt / Equity |
| Debt / EBITDA | Total Debt / EBITDA |
| Interest coverage | EBIT / Interest expense |
| Net debt | Total Debt − Cash & equivalents |

#### 4. Efficiency / Activity

| Ratio | Formula |
|---|---|
| Asset turnover | Revenue / Avg Total Assets |
| Inventory turnover | COGS / Avg Inventory |
| Days sales outstanding (DSO) | Receivables × 365 / Revenue |
| Days inventory | Inventory × 365 / COGS |
| Days payables outstanding (DPO) | Payables × 365 / COGS |
| **Working capital cycle** | DSO + Days inventory − DPO |

#### 5. Valuation

| Ratio | Formula |
|---|---|
| P/E | Price / EPS |
| P/B | Price / Book value per share |
| EV/EBITDA | Enterprise Value / EBITDA |
| EV/Sales | EV / Revenue |
| Dividend yield | DPS / Price |
| PEG | P/E / Earnings growth % |

### DuPont decomposition (high-yield)

```
ROE = (Net Profit / Sales) × (Sales / Assets) × (Assets / Equity)
    =  Net Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier
```

Identifies WHY ROE is high or low: profitability, efficiency, or leverage.

### Cash flow quality red flags

- **CFO < Net Profit consistently** → earnings not converting to cash → working-capital build-up suspicious.
- **Capex >> Depreciation** for years → either growth or low-quality capitalisation.
- **Negative FCF + rising debt** → unsustainable expansion.
- **Dividends > FCF** → funded by debt or asset sales.
- **One-time items** appearing every quarter → not really one-time.

### Off-balance-sheet liabilities

- **Operating leases** (now mostly on-BS under Ind AS 116, but still check)
- **Contingent liabilities** — pending litigation, guarantees
- **Letters of comfort** to subsidiaries
- **Recourse obligations** on factored receivables

### Common-size statements

Express each P&L line as % of revenue and each BS line as % of total assets — makes year-on-year and peer comparisons easy.

## 🧠 Memory Hooks

- **Three statements: P&L (period), Balance (snapshot), Cash Flow (period).**
- **EBITDA = Earnings Before Interest, Tax, D&A.** Adds back non-cash to focus on operating cash generation.
- **FCF = CFO − Capex.** What's actually available.
- **DuPont: NPM × ATO × EM = ROE.** Three drivers explain ROE — margin, turnover, leverage.
- **Working capital cycle = DSO + Days Inventory − DPO.** Lower is better.
- **CFO consistently < Net Profit = warning sign.**
- **Interest coverage = EBIT / Interest.** > 4-5× = comfortable; < 2× = stressed.
- **Common-size statements** are the cheapest way to spot trends + outliers.
