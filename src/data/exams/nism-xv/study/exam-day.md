# XV Exam Day Checklist

## Night before

- **Re-read [Number Sheet](./number-sheet) + [Memory Hooks](./memory-hooks).** No new material.
- Skip a fresh practice mock.
- Light dinner; 7-8 hours sleep; caffeine OK; no alcohol.
- Pack:
  - Photo ID (Aadhaar / PAN / DL — same one as registration)
  - Admit card (printout or PDF)
  - Pen (centre may not allow your own — verify)
  - **No** smart watch / mobile / notes — locker provided
  - Water bottle (allowed in lobby)

## Morning of

- **Arrive 30 min early.** ID + biometric verification takes 10 min.
- Glance at [Number Sheet](./number-sheet) in the lobby.
- 2 min calm breathing before screen unlock.

## During the 180 minutes

### Time strategy

- **180 min for 100 Qs = 108 sec / Q on average.**
- More breathing room than V-A or VIII (which are 120 min).
- Plan: **60 min for the first pass** at ~36 sec / Q (skim, answer obvious).
- **80 min for second pass** on flagged questions (numericals, valuation, ratios, technical patterns).
- **40 min reserved** for review + the 10-15 toughest you skipped entirely.

### Reading discipline

- **Read every option.** Especially in valuation + ratios — multiple options may be partially right.
- **Watch for "NOT", "EXCEPT", "INCORRECT"** in the stem.
- **Flag-and-skip aggressively** — a 30-second tussle on Q 12 costs the same as Q 95 you don't reach.

### Negative marking math

| Confidence | Decision |
|---|---|
| You know it | Answer (+1). |
| Narrowed to 2 options | Answer. EV ≈ +0.375. |
| Narrowed to 3 options | Answer. EV ≈ +0.165. |
| 4-option blind | EV ≈ +0.0625. **Skip first pass; revisit at end.** |

### Topic-by-topic order

If you can navigate freely (you can on Schoolnet):

1. **Pass 1: Chs 1, 2, 3, 5, 9, 11, 13, 14** — vocabulary + memory questions, fast.
2. **Pass 2: Chs 4, 6, 7, 12** — research framework + industry + governance + risk-return.
3. **Pass 3: Chs 8, 10, 15** — financial analysis + valuation + technical (the heaviest).

### Common pitfalls in the moment

- **Misapplying CAPM** — using nominal risk-free rate with real cash flows or vice versa.
- **DCF terminal-value blunder** — choosing g > long-term GDP growth.
- **Sharpe vs Treynor confusion** — Sharpe for any portfolio; Treynor only for well-diversified.
- **Bond price + yield** — always inverse; don't get fancy.
- **Candlestick patterns** — Hammer ≠ Shooting Star; Engulfing requires the second candle to fully cover the first.
- **RSI overbought** — overbought ≠ "sell now"; trends often persist.

## After

- **Don't compare answers** with others outside.
- **Result is instant** — Schoolnet shows pass/fail at submit.
- Score sheet emailed within minutes.

## If you fail

- Cool-off 24 hours.
- Re-attempt window opens immediately on NISM portal.
- Identify the 2-3 weakest topics from your score breakdown; redo those chapters + topic-drill mocks.

## If you pass

- Certificate available on NISM portal within 30 days.
- **Validity: 3 years.** CPE programme to renew.
- NISM-XV is a recognised SEBI credential — required for SEBI RA registration. Update LinkedIn.

---

**Goal: walk out with at least 70 marks.** That's a clear pass with margin.
